**Glossary of Key Concepts in Our Work**

**Singularity (as we define it here)**

Not simply the moment when AI surpasses human intelligence (as in Kurzweil's framing), nor the mathematical or physical singularity where known laws break down.\
In our vision:

**Singularity** is the moment when human systems, values, and technologies align so closely that the distinction between human intent and system behavior dissolves.

- It is the point where means and ends no longer require the friction of incentives, deception, or control --- because intelligence (augmented by AI) knows the right direction and pursues it without misalignment or waste.
- It represents not the obliteration of the human, but the elevation of collective wisdom --- a merging of our highest aspirations with our most powerful tools.
- Singularity is *not* the endpoint, but the threshold of a new kind of moral and intellectual existence: where our social, economic, technological, and ecological systems function as one integrated fabric.

**The Basket**

A metaphor for what we choose to carry forward through history's storms --- our collective values, our cultural treasures, our shared humanity.\
- The basket represents resilience, adaptability, and moral continuity.\
- A well-woven basket can hold the fruits of progress without spilling its ethical core.

**The Arrow**

Our trajectory --- individually and collectively.\
-  The *Still Arrow* refers to humanity's long, often frozen arc: the myths, institutions, and systems that once gave direction but now resist change.\
- The *Thin Arrow* refers to your personal journey: attempts to veer from inertia, met with friction and constraint.\
- The *Caged Arrow* represents how our myths, systems, and incentives limit the full release of our potential.

**The Castle**

A symbol of our collective structures at various scales --- from families to tribes to nations to planetary systems.\
- The castles we build can protect, or they can isolate and oppress.\
- Part of our work is to examine which walls are worth keeping, and which must come down.

**The Wildfire**

A symbol for runaway forces --- technologies, ideologies, or natural dynamics --- that, uncontained, can destroy what we value.\
- AI, synthetic biology, nuclear weapons, and climate change are modern wildfires that demand new firebreaks (ethical, technical, social).

**The Commons (Old and New)**

Shared spaces --- ecological, informational, digital, cultural --- where collective value is created or preserved.\
- The *New Commons* includes not just land and knowledge, but developer communities, ethical data pools, open algorithms, and tools of collective governance.
