_First you will come to the Sirens, who enchant all who come near them._  
--- **Homer**, _Odyssey_ XII

On a bright afternoon in Florida, in the spring of 2016, a car was driving itself down a divided highway.

Ahead, a truck turned across the road, its trailer broad and white.

The car's cameras looked at the white trailer against the bright sky and found nothing that needed stopping for. The company would later say that neither the system nor the man inside had noticed it. The brakes were never applied, and the man did not survive.

Odysseus heard the Sirens and lived, because he had made sure that if his own judgment failed, someone else's would hold. That afternoon, the machine's judgment and the man's failed together.
