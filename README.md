# The Path of Many Arrows

## An Odyssey Past Stillness and Singularity

**Publishing sources for a civilizational inquiry into why we need better ways of seeing, organizing, and becoming.**

[Read the current web preview](https://guten.ink/poma/)

*The Path of Many Arrows* asks why civilization needs better design. It follows the forces that shape human becoming across bodies, institutions, technologies, stories, and worlds—and asks how intelligence might move beyond inherited forms without abandoning life, plurality, or meaning.

The work is currently conceived as three connected volumes. Its web edition can grow incrementally while preserving the structure of the larger manuscript.

## Repository purpose

This repository is intended to support publication of selected material through Quarto and related formats. It may contain:

- publication-ready Markdown or Quarto source files;
- book structure and rendering configuration;
- approved images and styles;
- scripts used to convert and normalize publication sources;
- generated or staged web-publication assets where appropriate.

The reviewed Word-to-Markdown conversion was completed and explicitly confirmed by the author on 2026-09-29. Files under `manuscripts/` are now the canonical editorial sources; archived Word files are frozen provenance. The publishing process reads the canonical manuscripts and produces Quarto representations without silently changing their prose. See the [manuscript workflow](MANUSCRIPT-WORKFLOW.md) and [source-of-truth declaration](archive/SOURCE-OF-TRUTH-DECLARATION.md). The earlier `draft/` migration plan is retired.

## Current publication

The launch edition presents a limited preview: front matter, an introduction, and the prologue. Additional material will be released as it is prepared for public reading.

## Companion work

[*The Living Stack*](https://guten.ink/livingstack/) is the field guide to this inquiry. Where *The Path of Many Arrows* asks why better civilizational design is needed, *The Living Stack* asks how we might begin building it without losing the humans it is meant to serve.
