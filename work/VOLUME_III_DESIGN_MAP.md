# Volume III: opportunity signals, design principles, builder primitives, and The Next Move

Prepared 4 October 2026 from the canonical manuscripts at repository revision `80ce1e7`. **Editorial assessment and proposals for review; no manuscript changes.** Current chapter numbering is used throughout. The separate Chapter 23 opening experiments are not treated as adopted text.

## Editorial judgment

**Keep the eleven-pair order. Strengthen the mechanisms and distinguish the jobs of the paired pieces.** Each chapter has a defensible subject and useful principles. There is no displaced design domain that warrants another chapter shuffle. The principal weakness is that a principle is often stated repeatedly while the mechanism that would make it work receives a list of desirable features. An interlude should take the reader further into implementation, tradeoffs, and failure recovery.

The drafts are particularly strong on bounded help, plural belonging, correction, stewardship, and resisting capture. Their next improvement should also show the positive capacity these arrangements create: more time, wider choices, dependable provision, deeper learning, healthier lives, material competence, and cultural participation. A volume about beneficial acceleration must offer more than eleven ways to constrain it.

The biggest substantive work lies in Economy's allocation and settlement, Commons' physical maintenance alongside data governance, Governance's fiscal argument, Care's delivery capacity, and Release's actual transfer of authority. These are developments within the accepted structure.

This is a grouped inventory of the **design content**, not a sentence-by-sentence fact-check or an instruction to retain every existing heading. The design-bearing sections of all 22 canonical numbered units were reviewed; their openings and historical material were consulted as context where needed. The earlier complete reading remains supporting provenance. Source identities, hashes, all section headings, and the scoped reading record are in [the audit](volume-iii-design-map-audit.json). The [eleven extracted Volume II signals](VOLUME_II_OPPORTUNITY_SIGNALS.md) remain the source definitions.

### How to read the assessments

- **Keep:** belongs here and supplies a strong principle or useful mechanism.
- **Develop:** already present and worth retaining, but needs a worked choice, allocation, responsibility, or test. This does not mean the subject was absent.
- **Qualify:** the idea belongs, but its present claim is too broad, technically loose, or dependent on an unestablished future.
- **Add:** a proposed addition or a specifically identified operational extension, not a claim about current manuscript content.
- **Placement:** identifies where the full treatment belongs; a shorter application can remain elsewhere.

A builder *primitive* here is a governable object, operation, permission, relationship, or workflow. “Be transparent” is a principle; a decision record with evidence, responsible actor, correction path, and retention rule is a primitive. The audit below makes that difference explicit without pretending the book needs a software specification at every turn.

## 2. Chapter-by-chapter inventory and assessment

### 23 — The Loom of Renewal / The Loom of Design

**Distinct tension:** help must cross the gap between available capacity and unmet need without creating a new command structure. **Gate:** Coordination Gap, 11 March 2029. The historical opening establishes the problem of creating workable order; the design must show an actual coordination gain.

Sources: [chapter](../manuscripts/volume-3/23-chapter.md), especially “From Institutions to Flows” through “Build the First Knot”; [interlude](../manuscripts/volume-3/23-interlude.md), “Goodwill Is Not Architecture” onward.

**Signal inheritance.** D: **S12** changes the condition of failed coordination rather than counting more activity; **S15** makes hidden need safely reportable; **S18** gives mutual obligation roles and support; **S20** replaces disconnected institutional handoffs with usable flows. S: **S13** protects refusal and recovery; **S16** bounds risky matches and emergency authority; **S19** treats the coordination fabric as a maintained commons.

**Existing chapter topics and principles**

- **Keep — Institutions as organizers of flows.** Trace care, food, information, permission, and responsibility across existing institutions; preserve useful institutional competence.
- **Keep — Need visibility with dignity.** Reveal only the information necessary to deliver help, with correction and withdrawal appropriate to the task.
- **Keep — Capacity without conscription.** An offer of time, skill, space, or equipment has limits; reliable helpers must not become inexhaustible public infrastructure.
- **Keep — Availability before unnecessary hierarchy.** Start with what can be done and who can do it; retain legitimate authority for clinical, emergency, and other consequential acts.
- **Develop — Small meshes and a wider neighborhood.** Organize care, food, repair, transport, learning, and translation around reachable mutual relevance; specify when a thread becomes a supported service.
- **Keep — A modest civic covenant.** Mutual obligation must survive inconvenience while preserving exit, rest, dissent, and protection from local coercion.
- **Develop — Coordination that learns upstream.** Repeated requests should expose structural failures; success includes reducing the underlying need for emergency improvisation.

**Existing interlude primitives**

- **Keep — Need and capacity cards.** Describe the request or offer, authorized viewers, urgency, limits, expiry, correction, and support required by the contributor.
- **Keep — Scoped roles.** Separate requester, contributor, dispatcher, verifier, steward, auditor, advocate, and institutional liaison; technical access does not confer civic authority.
- **Keep — Contextual trust gradients.** Match verification to the action and risk; no universal reputation rank substitutes for relevant competence.
- **Keep — Boundaries and sunsets.** Define operating area, data limits, temporary powers, renewal authority, and an end to emergency access.
- **Develop — Routing and match review.** Make urgency, continuity, safety, fairness, and helper load explicit; both sides can reject an unsuitable match.
- **Keep — Feedback and structural escalation.** Record whether help actually arrived and what remained unresolved; support honest reporting without converting every mistake into identity.
- **Develop — Local federation and civic interfaces.** Specify how clinics, municipalities, schools, and community groups exchange limited state, escalate, and maintain the shared service.
- **Develop — Stewardship, failure register, and institutional backup.** Fund maintenance and handle fraud, burnout, exclusion, misrouting, privacy failures, and capture; the oath summarizes these duties rather than implementing them.

**Add / extend**

- **Explicit acceptance and handback:** a request passes from offered to accepted to delivered, failed, or returned. Someone retains responsibility until the next responsible party accepts it. The draft names handoffs; this makes their completion inspectable.
- **Scarcity and overload policy:** decide what happens when there are three helpers and twelve needs. Publish the prioritization rule, unresolved queue, and path into paid or institutional capacity.
- **Continuity without the platform:** provide a usable phone, paper, or in-person route and reconcile it with the digital record. Measure help delivered, unmet demand, burden, and dignity against the old arrangement.

**Placement / qualification.** Keep essential consent and commons protections here, but leave personal delegation detail to 24, mature commons governance to 27, and federation/jurisdiction machinery to 29. Emergency permission should expire; the audit record may need a different retention period. “This is not ideology” overstates the covenant's neutrality: its values should be openly argued. The new personal opening, if adopted later, will require replacing the current hall-cast callbacks.

**The Next Move — Make help arrive.** Begin with one recurring failure close enough to understand: a ride, translation, repair, or missed handoff. Join those already doing the work and make one dependable connection, with a backup and a way to decline. Let the result reveal whether the real next task is better routing or a service that ought to be funded. The invitation is to make obligation dependable.

### 24 — The Citizen's Dilemma / The Developer's Dilemma

**Distinct tension:** beneficial delegation can accumulate into authority over a life. **Gate:** Delegated Life, 11 September 2031. The chapter's unit is the person living with assistance, not the constitution of the whole mesh.

Sources: [chapter](../manuscripts/volume-3/24-chapter.md), “Agency Is Not a Button” onward; [interlude](../manuscripts/volume-3/24-interlude.md), “Help Without Custody” through “The Developer's Oath.”

**Signal inheritance.** D: **S13** makes choices usable and recovery affordable; **S17** prevents the personal model becoming a cage; **S21** makes assistance support judgment. S: **S15** exposes inferences for correction; **S16** bounds consequential interventions; **S20** makes portability and plural provision structural; **S22** permits pause, review, and revocation before convenience hardens into jurisdiction.

**Existing chapter topics and principles**

- **Keep — Agency beyond menus.** Intelligibility, feasible alternatives, time, refusal, reversibility, and appeal make choice real.
- **Keep — Bounded delegation.** Permission to schedule, recommend, or monitor does not silently authorize decisions about treatment, values, identity, or political judgment.
- **Keep — Trust through answerability.** Inspectability, failure disclosure, oversight, correction, and exit matter more than benevolent branding.
- **Keep — Inspect and outgrow the model.** People can inspect consequential claims, add context, challenge inferences, and distinguish past states from present identity.
- **Keep — Plural belonging and opacity.** Separate contexts and preserve areas of life that do not need to become inputs.
- **Develop — Help that can leave.** Revocation, switching, and reduced assistance must preserve essential services and supported dependence where needed.
- **Qualify — Proportionate intervention.** Evidence, urgency, rights, and accountability govern exceptions; the path back to agency is part of the intervention.
- **Keep — Dignity under recommendation.** Protect non-optimized paths, creative work, rest, grief, and meanings the recommendation system cannot price.

**Existing interlude primitives**

- **Develop — Consent and delegation records.** Scope, duration, actor, purpose, downstream use, renewal, revocation, and the consequences of refusal become enforceable settings and records.
- **Keep — Personal-model inspection surface.** Show meaningful claims, their sources, confidence, uses, and correction status rather than dumping an unintelligible technical export.
- **Develop — Inference lifecycle.** Expire or quarantine stale labels, distinguish history from current state, and propagate accepted corrections into decisions.
- **Keep — Consequential-decision explanation and review.** Scale explanation to stakes; identify responsibility, alternatives, uncertainty, and a real challenge route.
- **Qualify — Automation boundaries.** Specify actions requiring accountable authorization or independent review; “human” alone is not a quality guarantee.
- **Keep — Anti-coercion interface rules.** No hidden refusal paths, punitive refusal scores, shame prompts, bundled consent, or manipulative defaults.
- **Develop — Governed objectives and builder oversight.** Expose optimization priorities and conflicts; give independent reviewers authority and resources to change deployments.
- **Keep — Exit, portability, and plural help.** Switch assistants and carry useful records without transporting every inference or losing basic access.

**Add / extend**

- **Permission budgets and receipts:** bind an agent's authority to actions, money, data, duration, and any subdelegation; show what it actually did and prevent downstream agents from silently expanding the grant.
- **A usable freedom test:** compare completion effort for accepting, refusing, correcting, and switching. Check whether a formally available alternative is affordable and accessible.
- **Positive agency:** show assistance creating an option the person previously lacked—learning, travel, participation, accessible communication—without treating independence from all tools as the goal.

**Placement / qualification.** Keep universal recourse here as a right; develop remedy institutions in 30 and civic credentials in 29. Revocation stops future authority; it cannot undo every completed action. Inspection of consequential claims is attainable without claiming a model can reveal a complete, faithful internal portrait. Do not promise deletion of every downstream effect or rely on a fabricated explanation of a decision.

**The Next Move — Keep a key.** Take one important delegation and make its boundaries real: what it may do, what it must ask, how it can be corrected, and how another helper can take over. Use the capability it releases for something you choose. The test is whether assistance enlarges your life while the authority remains yours to grant.

### 25 — Beyond the Invisible Hand / The Living Ledger

**Distinct tension:** productive abundance and recognized contribution do not automatically become usable provision. **Gate:** Dividend Divide, 11 March 2034. The historical braid supports examining distribution and institutional design; a universal disappearance of desk work is not needed.

Sources: [chapter](../manuscripts/volume-3/25-chapter.md), “When Price Fails” onward; [interlude](../manuscripts/volume-3/25-interlude.md), “The Invisible Custodian” and the ledger sections.

**Signal inheritance.** D: **S12** changes the economic purpose from activity to provision; **S18** supports necessary contribution and reciprocal obligations; **S19** replenishes the commons and protects reserves. S: **S13** separates livelihood and recovery from obedience or approved productivity; **S15** detects need not expressed as purchasing power; **S20** changes the allocation and accounting architecture rather than decorating the old price signal.

**Existing chapter topics and principles**

- **Keep — Price and money as useful, incomplete representations.** Preserve their coordinating functions while making excluded costs, needs, and obligations visible.
- **Qualify — Reserves versus hoarding.** Protect household and civic buffers; challenge specific bottlenecks, exclusion, and political capture rather than treating every large financial balance as idle material.
- **Keep — Need-sensitive provisioning.** Detect shortages, care burdens, unused capacity, and ecological limits without turning lives into inventory.
- **Keep — Contribution beyond wages.** Support care, repair, art, learning, and stewardship through money, access, relief, or recognition as appropriate; no universal worth score.
- **Develop — Transactions within flows.** Evaluate what exchanges do to resilience, regeneration, access, and future capacity, alongside ordinary balances and obligations.
- **Develop — Banks and businesses as provision organizers.** Finance and enterprise can organize useful capacity; explain how risk, capital, payment, accountability, and losses work.
- **Keep — Basic provision and voluntary contribution.** Essential access must not depend on a contribution balance; livelihood includes belonging and purpose without prescribed usefulness.
- **Qualify — Abundance with agency.** Automation can free time and improve provision, but neither meaningful work nor concentrated ownership disappears automatically.

**Existing interlude primitives**

- **Develop — Breadboard connectors.** xFin, xMed, and xGaia connect internal experiments to external payments, professional obligations, and ecological consequences. Treat these as proposed interfaces, not evidence of achieved solvency or validated medicine.
- **Keep — Separate contribution and need records.** Record useful events with context, access limits, correction, and expiry; protect anonymity and unquantified contribution.
- **Qualify — Flow graphs and DAG provenance.** Trace dependencies and transformations. Economic circulation can cycle; an acyclic event history is one representation, not a complete economy or proof of causal benefit.
- **Develop — Service credits and plural value media.** State what a credit entitles its holder to, who supplies it, where it travels, and when it expires; do not make food or care conditional on earning it.
- **Develop — Reserves and circulation mechanisms.** Expiry, demurrage, reinvestment incentives, and anti-concentration rules are options requiring contextual justification and protection for prudent reserves.
- **Keep — Plural value registers.** Keep care, ecology, material provision, culture, and resilience visible without inventing one moral exchange rate.
- **Develop — Private records with public accountability.** Role access, selective disclosure, aggregate reporting, fraud review, and correction must protect people while exposing institutional performance.
- **Develop — Federated, contestable ledger governance.** Define validators, category changes, local/global translation, appeals, conflicts, exit, and who can override a mistaken allocation.

**Add / extend**

- **A real allocation and settlement layer:** distinguish recognition, enforceable claim, reserved supply, actual delivery, and payment. Address shortages, prioritization, double-counting, defaults, and who absorbs losses. A graph is not a funding source.
- **A distributive claim on automation:** examine shared ownership, public or cooperative stakes, dividends, and negotiated sharing of productivity gains. The chapter currently discusses abundance and basic income more clearly than ownership of the source of abundance.
- **A funded provision floor with plural choice:** show how essentials are paid for during transition, including nonparticipants and people unable to contribute. Compare a proposed mechanism with an ordinary cash/public-service alternative before celebrating novelty.

**Placement / qualification.** Keep economic distribution here; put the legitimacy and administration of taxation in 29. Do not mistake ledger design for economic theory, or contribution provenance for validated downstream impact. Money, financial claims, real stores, productive assets, and rent-extracting chokepoints need separate treatment; the apple metaphor does not establish their equivalence. The [Bank of England's account of money creation](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/quarterly-bulletin/2014/q1/money-creation-in-the-modern-economy) is a useful factual check against a banking model that treats deposits merely as stored objects.

**The Next Move — Give abundance somewhere to go.** Follow one actual productivity gain to its destination: profit, lower prices, shorter work, better service, care, or a shared reserve. Help design a claim that makes some of that gain dependable for people who need it. Start small enough to show who pays and who benefits; keep basic dignity outside the contribution ledger.

### 26 — Minds in Harmony / Signal and Story

**Distinct tension:** shared evidence must permit disagreement while powerful mediation changes what people can perceive and learn. **Gate:** Mediated World, 11 September 2036. Harmony should mean the ability to reason together, not compulsory agreement.

Sources: [chapter](../manuscripts/volume-3/26-chapter.md), “Shared Reality as Commons” onward; [interlude](../manuscripts/volume-3/26-interlude.md), “Signal Before Story” onward.

**Signal inheritance.** D: **S14** restores context and voices lost in transmission; **S15** separates observation, interpretation, and authority; **S17** protects correction of models and shared stories; **S21** develops discernment under abundant assistance. S: **S19** funds and governs shared epistemic infrastructure; **S22** gives attention, deliberation, and consequential sharing room for reconsideration.

**Existing chapter topics and principles**

- **Keep — Shared reality as a commons.** Maintain common records and methods that make disagreements intelligible without demanding identical opinions.
- **Keep — Interpretation beyond information access.** Supply context, uncertainty, counterargument, and the distinction between fact, knowledge, judgment, and values.
- **Develop — Education as judgment formation.** Teachers and AI tutors should cultivate reasoning, productive struggle, transfer, and curiosity; factual knowledge still matters.
- **Develop — Media as epistemic infrastructure.** Give journalism, public records, local media, recommendation, and correction explicit public responsibilities and viable support.
- **Keep — Accountable early warning.** “Practical prophets” need evidence, uncertainty, dissent protection, independent review, and a route from warning to action.
- **Keep — Provenance and correction.** Preserve origins and revisions; let correction travel with the original claim and make updating possible without humiliation.
- **Qualify — Plurality, attention, and protective friction.** Preserve different experiences and meanings while evaluating factual claims; protections must themselves be contestable.
- **Keep — Rehearsal for learning and wiser disagreement.** Shared simulations reveal assumptions and tradeoffs; they do not settle political values or replace lived evidence.

**Existing interlude primitives**

- **Keep — Claim and evidence records.** Distinguish observation, inference, interpretation, uncertainty, source, and the action a claim can responsibly support.
- **Qualify — Provenance and authenticity mechanisms.** Signed capture, custody, transformation history, and synthetic-content labels support verification; they do not establish that the depicted event or interpretation is true.
- **Develop — Layered claim status.** Authenticity, evidential strength, context, disputed interpretation, and review state require separate fields rather than one truth/confidence badge.
- **Keep — Versioned public records and correction propagation.** Preserve changes, attach retractions to citations and reposts where possible, and notify consequential downstream users.
- **Develop — Attention and recommendation controls.** Source comparison, slow modes, targeting disclosure, proportionate sharing friction, and transparent editorial decisions need accountable operators.
- **Keep — Cognitive and tutoring scaffolds.** Argument maps, explain-back, counterexamples, hints, uncertainty displays, and human teaching support active understanding.
- **Keep — Narrative and warning environments.** Separate disagreement about measurements, models, values, memory, and authority; grade warnings and track what action followed.
- **Develop — Epistemic recourse and anti-manipulation response.** Handle forgery, impersonation, synthetic crowds, contextual distortion, and reputation repair without making the verifier unchallengeable.
- **Develop — Plural epistemic stewardship.** Libraries, researchers, media, communities, and independent reviewers share responsibilities; generated art/story raises consent and provenance questions, not automatic illegitimacy.

**Add / extend**

- **Independence of corroboration:** five reports derived from the same source are one evidential lineage, not five witnesses. Mark dependency, uncertainty calibration, and what an unsigned but corroborated witness can establish.
- **A correction delivery test:** check who received the wrong claim and which decisions used it; measure whether correction reached those people and systems, not merely whether it was posted.
- **A positive learning test:** show learners using assistance to investigate, create, and solve a new problem. Test transfer where independence is the learning goal; judge permanent accessibility tools by usable agency, not removability.

**Placement / qualification.** Keep truth/evidence handling here; long-term cultural custody belongs in 32, legal remedies in 30, and deployment simulation in 33. Attention deserves full treatment here, a short commons application in 27. Replace “not censorship; sanitation” with the actual authority, appeal, proportionality, and dissent protections: the analogy does not remove the risk. The [C2PA explainer](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.2/explainer/Explainer.html) explicitly distinguishes provenance from factual truth. Lack of credentials is not a verdict against a witness.

**The Next Move — Make changing your mind easier.** Choose a shared question and build a small habit around its evidence: preserve the source, name what would change the conclusion, and carry a correction back to the people who heard the first account. A school, newsroom, library, family, or civic group can do this. The gain is a stronger capacity to learn together, not a badge for being right first.

### 27 — The Reclaimed Commons / Streams and Sanctuaries

**Distinct tension:** making shared dependencies visible can protect them or make them easier to appropriate. **Gate:** Intelligence as Infrastructure, 11 March 2039. Compute belongs inside obligations to land, water, energy, knowledge, and people.

Sources: [chapter](../manuscripts/volume-3/27-chapter.md), “The Cost of Invisible Substrates” onward; [interlude](../manuscripts/volume-3/27-interlude.md), “Streams and Sanctuaries” onward.

**Signal inheritance.** D: **S15** combines sensing with protection from the consequences of visibility; **S19** turns shared dependency into durable stewardship. S: **S14** restores place-bound and excluded knowledge; **S16** addresses depletion, emergency use, and irreversible exposure; **S18** assigns sustainable maintenance obligations; **S20** supplies public fallback and resists infrastructure lock-in.

**Existing chapter topics and principles**

- **Keep — Name and maintain the substrate.** Soil, water, biodiversity, public health, knowledge, trust, software, and infrastructure are load-bearing dependencies.
- **Qualify — Commons with boundaries.** Shared governance is neither unmanaged access nor necessarily universal access; different resources require different rules.
- **Keep — Data without exposure.** Distinguish public, personal, community, ecological, genomic, institutional, and protected knowledge, including data that should never be collected.
- **Keep — Nature beyond scenery or a service score.** Treat ecological systems as conditions of civilization while retaining their cultural, relational, and non-instrumental value.
- **Develop — Intelligence infrastructure with reciprocal obligations.** Account for compute's material demand, public benefit, access, resilience, and community authority.
- **Keep — Governed openness against enclosure.** Access, reciprocity, licensing, benefit sharing, portability, and public alternatives must fit the resource and rights-holders.
- **Keep — Instruments with situated knowledge.** Dashboards and embodied observation can correct each other; Indigenous authority is not decorative consultation.
- **Develop — Intergenerational stewardship and sanctuary.** Give maintenance, future claims, protected places, regenerative intervals, and limits enforceable form; watch capture, stagnation, and metric substitution.

**Existing interlude primitives**

- **Develop — Purpose-bound trusts and steward roles.** Specify duties, beneficiaries, access authority, conflicts, benefit return, succession, and redress; a database or label does not create a trust relationship by itself.
- **Keep — Community-specific data authority.** Respect collective protocols alongside individual rights; some knowledge is not available for collection or sharing.
- **Keep — Genomic and ecological use classifications.** Separate clinical, research, commercial, policing, insurance, public-warning, and sensitive-location uses; acknowledge family implications and re-identification risks.
- **Keep — Tiered access and temporary exceptions.** Define public aggregate, steward, research, operational, emergency, restricted, and no-collection/no-access states.
- **Develop — Purpose covenants and lineage.** Record permitted uses, derived-use restrictions, consent origin, retention, withdrawal, and remedies; metadata alone cannot enforce downstream conduct.
- **Develop — Benefit-sharing agreements.** Specify resources, access, capacity, recognition, or funds that return to contributors and custodians, with a way to verify delivery.
- **Qualify — Privacy-preserving tools and federated learning.** Different mechanisms protect different things; “data stays local” and “synthetic” do not independently establish privacy.
- **Keep — Incident response and the duty to share.** Contain harm, identify downstream use, provide remedy, and release urgent public-interest warnings at the necessary resolution.
- **Develop — Anti-enclosure infrastructure.** Open interfaces, alternative providers, public fallback, audit, and enforceable access rules reduce capture; none makes capture impossible.

**Add / extend**

- **A resource-specific stewardship account:** name the stock or service, safe use constraints, replenishment, maintenance budget, accountable operators, and enforcement. An aquifer, a software library, and a language archive cannot share one depletion model.
- **Dependable access to critical intelligence:** test whether public-interest users can obtain and switch essential compute/model services under outage, scarcity, or vendor withdrawal. This gives the infrastructure Gate a positive access obligation.
- **A privacy threat-and-guarantee table:** for each use, state who may learn what and what remains exposed. Separate secure computation, disclosure proofs, noisy aggregates, and local learning instead of presenting them as interchangeable protections.

**Placement / qualification.** Genomic commons governance belongs here; clinical interpretation belongs in 31. Archive-specific permissions belong in 32. Keep attention/trust as examples here after 26's full treatment. Distinguish expiry of access from retention of accountability records. Rootedness should become supported relationships and authority, not a generalized Indigenous aesthetic. [FNIGC identifies OCAP as specifically First Nations principles](https://fnigc.ca/ocap-training/); do not apply that name indiscriminately to all Indigenous peoples. [NIST SP 800-226](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/226/final) is a useful technical basis for discussing differential-privacy guarantees and implementation hazards, not a certificate for the whole commons design.

**The Next Move — Take a share of the stewardship.** Begin with a shared dependency you actually use, preferably through an existing legitimate stewardship body. Learn who maintains it, who bears its costs, and whose authority is being missed. Help secure one continuing obligation—maintenance, access, replenishment, or a protected boundary—with resources behind it. Belonging to a place becomes a practice of keeping it habitable.

### 28 — The Reimagined Workshop / Neufacturing

**Distinct tension:** increasingly programmable production must remain accountable to bodies, materials, and the object's afterlife. **Gate:** Programmable Production, 11 September 2041. The chapter's promise is useful material capability with less drudgery and dependence.

Sources: [chapter](../manuscripts/volume-3/28-chapter.md), “The Cost of Industrial Abstraction” onward; [interlude](../manuscripts/volume-3/28-interlude.md), “From Factory to Cell” onward.

**Signal inheritance.** D: **S16** matches production power to safety and containment; **S19** maintains materials, tools, and shared capacity; **S20** builds repair and adaptation into objects. S: **S12** distinguishes useful provision from throughput; **S18** carries responsibility through suppliers, makers, users, and recovery; **S22** makes a justified refusal or pause part of competent production.

**Existing chapter topics and principles**

- **Keep — The object as a chain of obligations.** Material origin, labor, energy, maintenance, repair, and disposal remain part of making after sale.
- **Keep — Start with need.** Repair, adapt, substitute, or avoid production before assuming a new object is required.
- **Keep — Appropriate scale.** Local rooms, regional hubs, and specialized global production have different strengths; local is not automatically safer or greener.
- **Keep — Repair and circular design.** Accessibility, parts, diagnostic access, disassembly, reuse, and remanufacture belong upstream of disposal.
- **Keep — Material memory without personal surveillance.** Passports track composition, safety, origin, certification, repair, and recovery without collecting an unnecessary biography of the user.
- **Qualify — Regenerative matter.** Evaluate feedstock, toxicity, energy, water, land, durability, and actual end-of-life conditions; biology and novelty do not supply ethical exemptions.
- **Keep — Human capability after automation.** Remove dangerous drudgery while supporting craft, judgment, apprenticeship, adaptation, art, and access to making.
- **Develop — Accountable supply meshes, standards, and workshop commons.** Safety, maintenance, access, funding, labor conditions, and commercial use need workable arrangements across scales.

**Existing interlude primitives**

- **Keep — Production-cell capability boundaries.** State what a local cell or regional hub may make, test, certify, repair, or refuse.
- **Develop — Parametric design envelopes.** Define permitted geometry/material/process variations and the evidence supporting them; customization is bounded by the actual use.
- **Keep — Method selection by consequence.** Compare additive, subtractive, molding, biological, and other methods against the job and lifecycle.
- **Keep — Material passport and lifecycle record.** Separate composition and design identity from the object's manufacture, maintenance, modification, transfer, and retirement events.
- **Develop — Repair and risk-based certification workflows.** Specify manuals, parts, operator competence, post-repair checks, liability, and high-risk escalation.
- **Qualify — Biofabrication and resource accounting.** Verify containment, feedstocks, energy, water, additives, and recovery rather than assuming a biological process is regenerative.
- **Keep — Supply meshes and open design libraries.** Provide alternative routes, substitution constraints, versions, known failures, attribution, and safe adaptation rules.
- **Develop — Waste feedback and cell governance.** Read losses and failures back into design; maintain machinery, fund wear, preserve access, prevent capture, and support skill transmission.

**Add / extend**

- **Evidence for the actual manufactured part:** a sound design file is insufficient without process qualification, material/batch traceability, inspection, and rules for changed parameters. The existing certification list needs a worked path.
- **Recall and physical recovery:** connect affected objects, responsible operators, repair/replacement, compensation, and material recovery. A software rollback cannot undo a failed load-bearing part.
- **A whole-life comparison with a service budget:** compare safe service delivered over time, repair burden, total resource use, and realistic recovery. Include tool maintenance, staff, insurance, and accessible pricing; efficiency alone can increase total consumption.

**Placement / qualification.** Keep broad livelihood distribution in 25 and shared resource budgets in 27; retain their specific consequences for making here. Do not make recyclability an unconditional veto on a safer, longer-lived object. Parametric customization and open design do not transfer a certification automatically. The clinician, regulator, process, and material context remain consequential.

**The Next Move — Give one thing another life.** Choose an object whose failure exposes an avoidable dependency. Work with competent repairers to restore, adapt, or replace it safely, then leave a useful record of the material, repair, and next recovery path. Where repair is blocked by design or policy, make that obstruction visible. The result should leave both an object and a community more capable.

### 29 — The Steward's Edge / Civic Meshes

**Distinct tension:** authority must be close enough to understand consequences and constrained enough to remain legitimate. **Gate:** Authority at Machine Speed, 11 March 2044. Civic institutions must govern rapid action without turning citizenship into continuous unpaid administration.

Sources: [chapter](../manuscripts/volume-3/29-chapter.md), “Governance After Trust Collapse” onward; [interlude](../manuscripts/volume-3/29-interlude.md), “The Person Is Not the Profile” onward.

**Signal inheritance.** D: **S18** makes civic roles and delegated responsibility sustainable; **S20** places authority, rights, and interfaces in a workable federation. S: **S13** makes participation and exit feasible; **S16** keeps machine action and emergencies within accountable limits; **S19** supports shared infrastructure; **S21** respects finite civic attention; **S22** enables review, amendment, and time-bounded exceptional powers.

**Existing chapter topics and principles**

- **Keep — Legitimacy through answerability.** Representation, performance, participation, inspection, fairness, and recourse must operate together.
- **Keep — Participation without civic conscription.** Offer direct, representative, delegated, deliberative, audit, and stewardship routes that ordinary people can realistically use.
- **Keep — Authority placed by consequence.** Local knowledge matters; cross-boundary effects, rights, resource constraints, and local capture may require wider authority.
- **Develop — Revocable delegation and accountable stewardship.** Scope civic authority, disclose conflicts, support roles, and prevent informal baronies.
- **Qualify — Public finance after automation.** The Silicon Tonnage Tax is a provocation; distinguish productive benefit, resource burden, infrastructure use, profits, and rents before choosing a tax base.
- **Keep — Layered standing and citizenship.** Legal status persists, while affected residents, rights-holders, communities, and future interests may have relevant standing beyond one passport category.
- **Keep — Machine responsibility with an address.** Trace accountable operators and institutions; autonomy must not erase liability or incident response.
- **Develop — Co-authorship protected from capture.** Rights floors, minority protections, independent appeal, amendment, and replaceable stewards make entering governance consequential. Canada is a workbench, not a universal model.

**Existing interlude primitives**

- **Keep — Separate identity, credential, role, right, delegation, and jurisdiction.** A credential verifies a claim; it does not confer unlimited authority or determine human worth.
- **Develop — Contextual credentials with recovery.** Minimal disclosure, offline alternatives, lost-wallet recovery, plural recognition, and public fallback protect practical access.
- **Keep — Membership and standing relationships.** Represent overlapping territorial, relational, professional, ecological, treaty, and affected-party claims without collapsing them into a master profile.
- **Develop — Delegation records.** Show scope, duration, chain, conflicts, revocation, limits on accumulation, and non-delegable rights.
- **Develop — Versioned civic rules and amendments.** Define proposals, quorum, decision authority, emergency powers, protections, change records, and rollback or transition.
- **Keep — Federation and dispute routing.** Shared interfaces, mutual recognition, rights floors, and escalation keep cross-mesh conflicts from becoming a citizen's scavenger hunt.
- **Qualify — Machine registries and fiscal measurements.** Tie consequential machine operations to responsible entities and proportionate measurement; avoid registering every trivial automation or equating compute with economic value.
- **Keep — Authority audit and anti-capture machinery.** Record decisions, evidence, delegation, dissent, review, conflicts, and external challenge while protecting private participants.

**Add / extend**

- **An adoption path inside real institutions:** show which existing authority can authorize a trial, which rights survive unchanged, what must be negotiated, and how a local initiative obtains durable legitimacy.
- **Participation capacity:** budget compensated participation, translation, accessibility, independent advice, and administrative support; use representative sampling where appropriate instead of assuming whoever attends represents everyone.
- **Separate fiscal instruments by purpose:** compare charges for infrastructure/externalities, taxation of income/profit/rents, and returns from public or shared ownership. Show incidence, avoidance, financing, and the effect on beneficial innovation before endorsing a machine levy.

**Placement / qualification.** Keep ownership and dividend design in 25; 29 decides who may authorize, collect, allocate, and challenge public claims. Personal agent permission belongs in 24; formal remedies belong in 30. Liquid delegation is one candidate arrangement, not the constitutional default. Revoking a delegation does not erase completed valid decisions. Affected-party standing, democratic membership, and Indigenous jurisdiction must remain distinct concepts.

**The Next Move — Put one rule within reach.** Choose a rule that governs a real shared activity and discover who can change it. Help establish a credible route for affected people to propose a revision, hear objections, decide, and review its effects. Give that process enough time, resources, and authority to matter. Co-authorship begins when a person's considered contribution can change the rule.

### 30 — Justice Before Judgment / Protocols of Integrity

**Distinct tension:** each component can obey its rules while the combined system injures a person. **Gate:** Justice Across the Chain, 11 September 2046. Justice must reach classifications and handoffs before harm, and still deliver enforceable repair afterward.

Sources: [chapter](../manuscripts/volume-3/30-chapter.md), “The Illusion of Neutral Systems” onward; [interlude](../manuscripts/volume-3/30-interlude.md), “Compliance Is Not Integrity” onward.

**Signal inheritance.** D: **S13** prevents a temporary mismatch becoming exclusion; **S17** opens classifications and fairness assumptions to challenge; **S20** evaluates the whole decision path rather than isolated compliant components. S: **S14** restores evidence and historical context; **S15** makes harms and errors reportable; **S16** limits cascading and irreversible consequences; **S18** funds responsibility and repair; **S21** gives reviewers actual comprehension and authority; **S22** permits holds and reconsideration before harm outruns appeal. This is the broadest mapping because the chapter expressly brings these mechanisms together, not because justice is a generic virtue applicable everywhere.

**Existing chapter topics and principles**

- **Keep — Categories are consequential judgments.** Inspect eligibility, risk, identity, and evidentiary classifications before calling the resulting procedure neutral.
- **Keep — Fairness begins upstream.** Evaluate data, proxies, categories, historical burden, consultation, and outliers rather than relying on a fair-looking average.
- **Keep — Evidence has a journey.** Distinguish source, custody, transformation, inference, uncertainty, and corroboration.
- **Keep — Due process must be usable.** Notice, reasons, evidence, response, independent review, assistance, timelines, and appeal should be accessible to the governed person.
- **Develop — Recourse can hold harm and provide repair.** Bridge essential support where justified, correct records, reverse decisions, compensate, and prevent recurrence.
- **Keep — Proportionate prevention.** Alter dangerous conditions and use the least restrictive adequate response; probability must not silently become guilt.
- **Keep — Restoration with accountability.** Distinguish honest error, negligence, and deliberate harm; repair is owed without requiring victims to forgive or reconcile.
- **Develop — Justice across systems and resources.** Trace machine responsibility, cross-jurisdiction harm, extraction, and missing standing; preserve dignity during investigation and maintain justice as accessible civic infrastructure.

**Existing interlude primitives**

- **Keep — Decision traces and evidence types.** Connect actor, authority, rule/model version, evidence, uncertainty, alternatives, review, and remedy to a consequential decision.
- **Develop — Explicit fairness claims and challenge cases.** State the selected fairness objective and tradeoffs; test rare, poorly represented, and administratively inconvenient lives.
- **Keep — A due-process case workflow.** Route notice, answer, independent review, appeal, correction, and remedy without requiring the person to diagnose the technical layer.
- **Develop — Proportionality and continuity rules.** Use urgency, evidence, reversibility, rights, alternatives, and time-to-harm to decide holds, provisional support, or escalation.
- **Keep — Postmortems and restorative pathways.** Identify the failure, responsible changes, harmed people, remedies, review date, and accountability beyond a completed ticket.
- **Qualify — Reliability engineering for justice.** Use redundancy, fail-safe modes, outage access, stress tests, and rollback while distinguishing tolerable operational errors from violations of rights.
- **Keep — Real human review and skeptical metrics.** Give reviewers time, independence, evidence, and power; test whether low appeals or rapid closures reflect exclusion rather than justice.
- **Develop — Portable appeals and integrity networks.** Ombuds, advocates, reviewers, and jurisdictions need interoperable cases, adequate funding, external challenge, and authority to secure remedies.

**Add / extend**

- **One accountable route to completion:** receiving a complaint creates a duty to route and track it through resolution, even when several bodies must decide it. Federation must not offload that work onto the harmed person.
- **A prespecified burden and remedy structure:** explain what the institution must show, what the claimant need not prove, who can authorize interim protection, and how remedy is funded. The draft names funds and support; this extension makes them usable.
- **Repair the affected class of cases:** after a systemic error, search for similarly affected people, including those who never appealed, notify them safely, and verify that records and downstream decisions changed.

**Placement / qualification.** Keep the underlying jurisdiction map in 29 and evidence authenticity foundations in 26; make this pair the home of enforceable remedy. “Fail open” applies only where provisional access is safer and legitimate; other systems need a safe stop. The phrase “error budget” must never imply a quota of acceptable injustice. Harm from a completed exposure, injury, or lost opportunity cannot always be reversed by rolling back software.

**The Next Move — Carry the appeal through to repair.** Choose one recurring decision that harms people when it goes wrong. Help create or strengthen a route from “this is wrong” to timely protection, independent review, and actual remedy. Follow the failure across the whole chain and repair similar cases. The invitation is to make a right usable by someone without the resources to become a heroic complainant.

### 31 — The Flow of Care / From Molecule to Moment

**Distinct tension:** increasingly precise representations must produce better care for a person whose life does not follow institutional boundaries. **Gate:** Long Care Horizon, 11 March 2049. The desired gain is health, function, relief, and supported living—not a larger monitoring burden.

Sources: [chapter](../manuscripts/volume-3/31-chapter.md), “The Failure of Protocol Medicine” onward; [interlude](../manuscripts/volume-3/31-interlude.md), “From Record to Pattern” onward.

**Signal inheritance.** D: **S15** receives symptoms, caregiver observations, and uncertain measurements without silencing them; **S18** makes care handoffs and caregiver obligations sustainable; **S20** organizes around a longitudinal person rather than visits and departments. S: **S13** preserves feasible choices, access, and goals; **S16** handles coupled interventions and escalation; **S17** keeps the clinical model open to contradiction; **S19** maintains care capacity; **S21** supports patient and clinician judgment under limited attention.

**Existing chapter topics and principles**

- **Keep — Protocols with contextual judgment.** Retain evidence-based standards while allowing justified exceptions, multimorbidity, and questions the usual pathway misses.
- **Keep — Whole-person interpretation over time.** Integrate physiology, medication, function, environment, social support, history, and what matters to the patient.
- **Keep — Trust, continuity, and presence.** Technology should make responsible attention more possible; relationship is a care capability, not decoration.
- **Develop — A patient–clinician–system loop.** Signals must reach interpretation, a shared decision, intervention, follow-up, and revision, with ways to reopen a mistaken conclusion.
- **Qualify — AI support with accountable authority.** Use better pattern recognition, synthesis, and intervention support without allowing responsibility to vanish into a model or a nominal human signature.
- **Keep — Uncertainty and caregiver knowledge as structured inputs.** Families can notice missing context; consent, disagreement, safety, and the patient's own voice remain essential.
- **Develop — Home, prevention, and precision with real support.** Monitoring and molecular insight must connect to action, respite, accessible care, and realistic household capacity.
- **Keep — Dignity, goals, and equity across aging.** Distinguish treatable decline, acceptable treatment burden, palliation, and ageist dismissal; protect patients and caregivers, including those outside data-rich settings.

**Existing interlude primitives**

- **Keep — Longitudinal state and temporal updates.** Separate fast physiology, slower trends, historical facts, current inferences, and values requiring conversation.
- **Qualify — Bounded clinical twins.** Use a model for specified questions with uncertainty, validation, provenance, and limits; it is neither a complete person nor an oracle.
- **Keep — Actionability-aware genomic interpretation.** Distinguish clinically supported findings, uncertain variants, research, ancestry limitations, family implications, and non-actionable information.
- **Keep — Multisource clinical signals.** Track calibration, missingness, context, reliability, and usefulness of labs, wearables, symptoms, and caregiver observations.
- **Develop — Medication state and organ coupling.** Reconcile what was ordered, dispensed, changed, held, and actually taken; assign review responsibility across clinicians and pharmacies.
- **Develop — Uncertainty and escalation pathways.** Link a signal to urgency, a recipient with capacity, alternative hypotheses, follow-up, and the consequences of inaction or overaction.
- **Keep — Patient/family access and clinical provenance.** Provide understandable, consented roles, corrections, goals, evidence trails, second opinions, and recourse.
- **Develop — Lifecycle validation and operational safety.** Prospective/local evaluation, subgroup performance, drift, adverse events, overrides, fallback, and rollback should cover the actual care workflow.

**Add / extend**

- **A staffed closed-loop care commitment:** every consequential alert or referral has an accepting recipient, response expectation, backup, and confirmation that action occurred. The draft asks the right capacity question; show its operating answer.
- **A patient-defined benefit and burden test:** assess function, symptoms, meaningful time, caregiver load, treatment effort, and adverse effects alongside predictive performance. Support changing goals and declining unwanted monitoring.
- **A path from discovery to dependable treatment:** show how a useful new diagnostic or therapeutic capability becomes validated, affordable, deliverable care. Include practical delivery and review, rather than making the advanced future only a better chart.

**Placement / qualification.** Shared genomic data governance belongs in 27; keep patient-specific interpretation here. Justice supplies generic recourse, but clinical time-to-harm warrants its own application. General population health and social conditions matter, yet this chapter should retain its healthcare center. Do not permanently confine 2049 AI to a 2026 advisory interface: allocate autonomy by demonstrated capability, stakes, authorization, and accountable care arrangements. [FDA's account of the 2025 IMDRF principles](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/artificial-intelligence-enabled-medical-devices/good-machine-learning-practice-medical-device-development-guiding-principles) supports lifecycle discipline; it does not validate a future whole-person twin. Proposed mechanisms cannot establish that a particular intervention would have saved the author's father.

**The Next Move — Keep the care connected.** Start with one place where a person's care becomes nobody's responsibility: a discharge, medication change, referral, or move home. With the patient and appropriate care team, establish who follows through, what change should prompt a response, and who provides backup. The aim is a person better supported in living, with fewer duties pushed silently onto the household.

### 32 — Threads of Continuity / Encoding the Eternal

**Distinct tension:** preserving an inheritance must leave the living free to reinterpret it and the absent protected from invented completion. **Gate:** Living Inheritance, 11 September 2051. A retrievable culture and a living culture are related achievements, not interchangeable ones.

Sources: [chapter](../manuscripts/volume-3/32-chapter.md), “What Should Survive?” onward; [interlude](../manuscripts/volume-3/32-interlude.md), “Storage Is Not Memory” onward.

**Signal inheritance.** D: **S14** recovers buried records and missing context; **S17** prevents ontology and inherited stories becoming permanent cages; **S19** treats memory and language as maintained inheritances. S: **S18** supports custodial duties and generational handoffs; **S20** makes preservation interpretable, repairable, and independent of one technical custodian.

**Existing chapter topics and principles**

- **Keep — Selective continuity.** Preserve knowledge, language, care, art, and warnings while retiring inherited domination; age alone confers no authority.
- **Keep — Memory in living use.** Speaking, teaching, cooking, practicing, adapting, and questioning carry culture beyond the archive.
- **Keep — Refactor the functions of old stories.** Belonging, grief, obligation, awe, restraint, and shared time deserve honest forms even when cosmological claims change.
- **Develop — Ritual as community practice.** Let meaningful thresholds and renewal grow from participants rather than impose a designer's ceremonial program.
- **Keep — Ethical archives and incompleteness.** Consent, provenance, restricted access, restoration limits, and truthful gaps protect memory from appropriation.
- **Keep — Language, place, and plural belonging.** Diaspora and translation can carry culture without dissolving its local authority, texture, and internal disagreement.
- **Keep — Trauma and art as consequential inheritances.** Preserve testimony and expressive pressure without making injury a compulsory identity or a script for future revenge.
- **Qualify — Long-duration and human-scale continuity.** Plan beyond a vendor or lifespan; protect meaning under advanced mediation without requiring a literal synthetic species split in 2051.

**Existing interlude primitives**

- **Keep — Context-rich memory objects.** Record source, relations, language, intended use, uncertainty, consent, restrictions, and authorized custodians.
- **Keep — Revisable ontologies and typed graph relationships.** Distinguish facts, testimony, resemblance, inference, disputed meanings, temporal validity, and prohibited inference.
- **Keep — Preserved artifacts with versioned interpretation.** Separate originals, transcription, translation, restoration, commentary, and synthetic reconstruction.
- **Keep — Cultural, linguistic, and ritual metadata.** Carry pronunciation, humor, setting, permissions, performance context, and living variations rather than only searchable text.
- **Keep — Explicit missingness and reconstruction limits.** Damage, unknown origin, restriction, deliberate silence, and trauma create different obligations; a machine must not silently complete them.
- **Develop — Forgetting, sealing, and withdrawal.** Distinguish personal protection, public-interest preservation, lawful retention, and the limitations of undoing prior model use.
- **Develop — Community archive governance and training permissions.** Specify custody, access, benefits, reconstruction, derivative use, and internal disputes; custody is not ownership.
- **Keep — Durable preservation and authenticity.** Open formats, replicas, checksums, migration, emulation, human-readable context, restricted access, and disaster recovery sustain a usable semantic commons.

**Add / extend**

- **Custodial succession with resources:** identify who can act when a custodian dies, a community institution changes, or a vendor closes; transfer permissions, knowledge, and maintenance funding together.
- **A living-transmission measure:** judge success partly by people who can use, teach, reinterpret, and create in the practice or language. Pair preservation with community-controlled apprenticeship and creative use.
- **An accountable way to contest custodians:** protect differing members, diaspora, younger generations, individual privacy, and evidence of public harm. “The community decides” cannot always identify a single uncontested voice.

**Placement / qualification.** Keep the philosophy and archive interfaces of reconstruction here; develop intimate interactive personas, grief-product incentives, and uncertain synthetic welfare in 33. Do not discard the author's personal reflections on continuity merely because they are not primitives. They give the design its stakes. Preserve the distinction between a cultural restriction, a legal prohibition, and a technically enforceable control. “Model unlearning” is not a promise that all copies and effects can be recalled.

**The Next Move — Leave something living.** Carry one meaningful piece of inheritance beyond storage: a story with its uncertainty intact, a practice taught, a language used, an artifact entrusted. Record how it may be shared and what must remain private or unfinished. Give the next custodian resources and room to change it. The point is continuation through another person's life.

### 33 — The Arrow's Release / Machines as Mirrors

**Distinct tension:** the founder must transfer authority without dropping responsibility for consequences. **Gate:** Successor's World, 11 March 2054. The final test is whether the work can be corrected, continued, or replaced by people who owe its maker no obedience.

Sources: [chapter](../manuscripts/volume-3/33-chapter.md), “The End of Certainty” onward; [interlude](../manuscripts/volume-3/33-interlude.md), “Not Resurrection” through “Before the Epilogue.”

**Signal inheritance.** D: **S12** asks whether the Still Arrow's underlying direction changed; **S16** carries responsibility through powerful deployment; **S17** leaves even Neuverse's models and purposes revisable; **S22** turns rehearsal and review into better action. S: **S13** preserves successors' feasible freedom to refuse or depart; **S18** prepares a supported handoff; **S19** leaves maintained commons; **S20** makes the design amendable, repairable, and capable of surviving its founder.

**Existing chapter topics and principles**

- **Keep — Orientation without certainty.** Designs are revisable proposals; the future can change conditions and expose the author's assumptions.
- **Keep — Prepared release.** Leave recourse, memory, tools, maintenance capacity, and usable instructions; neither domination nor neglected obligations is a gift.
- **Keep — Care with boundaries.** Powerful protection must preserve refusal, appeal, correction, and room for unplanned life.
- **Qualify — The humane singularity.** Retain the question of accountable civilizational capability as the author's philosophical proposal; it is not a technically established threshold or forecast.
- **Qualify — Simulation before consequential scale.** Rehearse failures and include affected people; passing a model cannot certify the real world.
- **Keep — Rooms and future co-authorship.** Preserve embodied encounter, plural deliberation, and the successor's right to become different.
- **Develop — Founder independence and failure pathways.** Amendment, repair, exit, fork, oversight, and responsibility must work without the original designer's favor.
- **Keep — Grief, basket, workbench, and release.** These carry motivation and inheritance; the personal loss may generate a useful tool without becoming a demand that successors preserve the wound or its vocabulary.

**Existing interlude principles and primitive candidates**

- **Keep — Reconstruction distinct from survival.** A responsive likeness does not establish the continuation of a person; separate trace, inference, performance, and unknown experience.
- **Keep — A model's frame must be inspectable.** Disclose assumptions, excluded people and variables, objectives, funding, uncertainty, and whose interests shape the representation.
- **Develop — Civic rehearsal environments.** Use alternative scenarios, edge cases, adversarial conditions, and affected people to challenge policy and system designs before wider deployment.
- **Qualify — “Do not test civilization in production.”** Retain the warning against reckless scale; translate it into proportionate evidence, bounded field trials, monitoring, and remedies. Some knowledge only comes from accountable real use.

- **Keep — Simulation returns to the world.** Models inform witness, touch, politics, consent, and grief; they must not claim jurisdiction over all their purposes.

**Add / extend**

- **A deployment evidence package:** intended use, benefit over the present arrangement, assumptions, limits, field evidence, protected participants, staged exposure, stop/expand criteria, incident owner, and resources for remedy. Simulation is one component.
- **A working Breadboard-to-world loop:** distinguish synthetic simulation, replay or shadow operation, consenting limited use, and wider adoption. Compare predicted and observed effects and change the design when they diverge. Test beneficial performance as seriously as containment.
- **A founder-independent handover:** demonstrate transfer of credentials, operational knowledge, assets, maintenance funds, amendment power, and responsibility for unresolved harms. Let successors make a consequential change the founder would not choose.
- **A persona use-and-welfare review:** separate duties to the represented person, living users, other people in the source material, and any plausibly experiencing system. Deletion, indefinite operation, replication, and shutdown cannot all be treated as ethically automatic.

**Placement / qualification.** Preserve the author's Mirror and emotional close. Move detailed archive storage/permissions to 32 and generic justice machinery to 30; keep their consequences here. Broader Breadboard architecture can be seeded in Theta and demonstrated across interludes, then brought together here. A far-future persona may call for new protections, but [Butlin and colleagues' indicator-based research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708) supports inquiry under uncertainty, not a declaration that the imagined Mirror is conscious. The current categorical claim that simulated beings do not experience harm conflicts with the interlude's later uncertainty: distinguish ordinary simplified representations from systems that plausibly warrant welfare concern.

**The Next Move — Hand over the keys.** Choose something you have helped build and prepare it to continue without your permission. Leave knowledge, resources, repair paths, and responsibility clearly assigned; then let others make a real decision you would not have made. Retain the obligations that are still yours while releasing authorship as a claim to rule. The last invitation is to become a useful ancestor.

## 4. Placement, compression, and the next design pass

### Give each mechanism a primary home

Several safeguards rightly recur. Repetition becomes costly when each chapter reintroduces the entire philosophy rather than applying it to a new consequence. The following are primary homes for the full explanation; they are not exclusive ownership of every mention.

| Pair | Primary design object and builder treatment |
|---|---|
| 23 | A need/capacity match and accepted handoff; reliable coordination across existing services. |
| 24 | A person's permissions, delegations, and consequential model; usable control and alternatives. |
| 25 | A contribution, economic claim, allocation, and settlement; who receives real provision and who bears loss. |
| 26 | A claim, source, interpretation, and correction; evidence handling and learning. |
| 27 | A shared resource, its custodial authority, constraints, and maintenance obligations. |
| 28 | A design, production process, physical artifact, and material afterlife; qualified making and repair. |
| 29 | Membership, standing, jurisdiction, delegated public authority, and rule amendment. |
| 30 | A contested consequential decision, its evidence, review, protection, and enforceable remedy. |
| 31 | A person's changing clinical state, care goals, interventions, and completed care handoffs. |
| 32 | A cultural record or practice, its context, permissions, custody, and living transmission. |
| 33 | A proposed intervention and its evidence of benefit, bounded trial, revision, and eventual succession. |

Provenance is reusable infrastructure, but an economic claim, a clinical observation, and a cultural permission are different objects. Explain the general relationship among sources, transformations, and responsible actors in Interlude 26; specialize it where consequences differ. [W3C PROV](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/) supplies a representation vocabulary, not a guarantee of truth, consent, justice, or correct economic attribution. Nothing here calls for one universal public ledger.

Theta can establish the common questions once: what improves, for whom, under whose authority, with what resources, how failure is noticed, who responds, and how a person obtains remedy. Later chapters should answer these questions with their own mechanisms. A clinical escalation rule, a manufacturing recall, and a cultural restriction must not become generic copies of “consent, audit, appeal.”

### What should move or lose its present framing

- **Move the full explanation, retain the local consequence.** Personal delegation belongs primarily in 24; resource custody in 27; public authority in 29; remedy in 30; durable memory in 32; deployment rehearsal and succession in 33. Short applications elsewhere earn their place when they change a design decision.
- **Make the paired pieces advance the argument.** Keep the chapter's serious design reasoning, human stakes, and institutional choices. Put detailed records, state transitions, permissions, interfaces, operating duties, and test cases in the interlude. This is not a division between an emotional chapter and a technical appendix.
- **Correct categorical promises.** A DAG does not deliver economic justice; a provenance mark does not prove truth; a model is not its subject; a digital rollback cannot undo every harm. Do not require every beneficial scaffold to disappear or describe all mediated attention management as neutral sanitation.
- **Keep the physical world present.** Commons cannot become only data governance; Making cannot stop at digital design files; Care cannot end at a better prediction. Maintenance, material limits, qualified production, staff, money, and time are design inputs.
- **Preserve philosophical work without mislabeling it.** The Mirror, the basket, grief, the Still Arrow, and cultural inheritance supply purposes and stakes. They need not masquerade as implementable primitives to belong.
- **Retire unvalidated forecast labels in the later prose pass.** Desk Exodus, Hall of Mirrors, Sovereign Grid, Silicon Tonnage Tax, Erased Perimeter, Synthetic Species Split, and Return of the Room still appear in inherited framing. Their presence does not supersede the accepted gates. In particular, a fiscal proposal or a species split cannot serve as an established forecast merely because it once titled a gate.

### Highest-value developments before condensation

1. **Economy:** work through one complete allocation—need, entitlement, resources, delivery, payment, shortfall, and loss-bearing—and compare it with a familiar alternative. Then show how ownership changes the destination of productivity gains.
2. **Commons and Making:** pair information rules with physical budgets, maintenance, resource-specific limits, inspection, and recovery. The material world must be able to disagree with its ledger.
3. **Governance and Justice:** show one legitimate route from an existing institution to a revisable arrangement, then one harmed person's route all the way through remedy. Public authority and operational access are different permissions.
4. **Care:** carry one advanced capability through actual access and supported treatment. Prediction earns its space when care reaches the patient and produces an outcome that matters to that person.
5. **Release:** show the Breadboard learning from bounded real use, and a founder handing over actual authority. Rehearsal is part of preparation; an agreeable simulation is not a certificate for society.

These priorities strengthen the substance before the planned condensation and voice pass. They do not require drafting eleven new treatises. A few fully worked mechanisms can replace many declarations of desirable qualities.

The gate dates describe later conditions under which designs will be tested. They do not postpone care, justice, memory, or succession until their chapter's year: all need appropriate provision from the initial implementation, with capabilities and obligations maturing together.
