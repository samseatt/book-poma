## Editorial choices worth reviewing

**History retains its own substance.** Each movement starts from evidence or a clearly identified transmitted story, rather than using an empire label as proof of a design principle. Kaveh is a character in the poem being composed around 1001; the scene is not presented as an event occurring in that year. The Roman–Parthian letters are known through an ancient account, not surviving diplomatic originals.

**Dates carry the qualifications without dominating the prose.** Examples: uncertain early Arsacid chronology; the 2 CE meeting following the 1 CE dispute; the 502–503 campaign following Kavad's position in 501; Abbasid consolidation in 751 after the 750 overthrow; the ICJ's 1952 jurisdiction decision; and Karim Khan's later Shiraz title and succession problem. These are explicit continuations, not events silently pulled onto the cutoffs.

**One source discrepancy was resolved conservatively.** The National Archives' Lee Resolution overview groups three committee appointments on June 11. The Library of Congress's [primary-document guide](https://guides.loc.gov/articles-of-confederation/digital-collections) distinguishes the June 11 resolution to form a confederation committee from appointment of its members on June 12. The manuscript describes the committee work without assigning all appointments to one day. The exact dates that matter to the argument remain June 7, July 2 and July 4.

**Rootedness has a specific beginning and several returns.** The bison example describes a long archaeological tradition and living Blackfoot knowledge, not a fabricated event in 250 BCE. The Creek, Lakota/Northern Cheyenne/Arapaho, Canadian First Nations and Mi'kmaq histories remain distinct. The optional 1752 treaty is included briefly in Release. The argument concerns the material and political conditions for living cultures to continue, not cultural imagery available for a new system to appropriate.

**A few alternatives were left out deliberately.** Bactria would dilute the first economic movement; the failed 1851 US–Persian treaty would crowd Truth's speech; Valerian's 260 capture and Wounded Knee in 1890 are not smuggled into earlier cutoffs. The human-genome draft remains an interlude candidate. The Cherokee/Chickamauga background is omitted from Renewal, which already has sufficient founding material. The full 2026 news account belongs to Theta and requires a separate check at the scene's date.

**Breadboard is a proposed method, not retrospective proof.** The new sections give concrete tests where useful, with outside funding, unpaid labour, authority and remedies visible. They do not claim Neuverse currently satisfies them, or that a simulation certifies civilizational outcomes. C4A's proposed renaming is recorded in the Theta list without applying it globally.

**The futures retain gains as well as difficulties.** The gates describe plausible accumulated conditions: useful agents, more accessible expertise, better production and care, more capable preservation and succession. They are not arrival dates for those capabilities. This pass does not validate every older technological assertion in the surrounding bodies or scenes.
