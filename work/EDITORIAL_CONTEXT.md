## Style and epistemic integrity

Use the full guide at `/Users/samseatt/projects/book_poma/_GPT_session_outputs/GPT_writing_and_style_guide.md`. The previous session's Carbon-Twain voice is valuable, but the author finds significant empty writing that can be condensed. Preserve substantive association, technical exposition, useful repetition, rhythm, humor, and emotional depth while removing prose that merely restates or decorates a completed thought. There is no arbitrary word-reduction target.

Carbon-Twain means technically serious, interdisciplinary, skeptical, humane, self-implicating, occasionally lyrical and funny, with changing registers. It is not Twain imitation or an instruction to put jokes everywhere. Do not homogenize passages, shorten every sentence, remove every parenthesis, or simplify technical vocabulary automatically. Set up a strong line and let it land; avoid explaining it afterward.

Past autobiographical scenes are factual to the precision claimed. Never invent dialogue, gestures, weather, sensory detail, motives, chronology, or what the earlier self supposedly knew. Future scenes are explicitly conjectural and must remain realistic. Distinguish evidence, interpretation, authorial conjecture, and analogy. Verify external claims and quotations before treating them as established; inherited text and packets do not validate themselves.
