# Volume III design principles and building patterns

6 November 2026. A working catalogue of the reconciled **Θ pair and all eleven numbered pairs**. It states the rules a reader can carry into another design or build, including principles implicit in the Neusphere proposals. It complements the opportunity-signal map and Next Moves; it does not replace them.

**Established** identifies a recognized method, pattern or disciplinary principle, sometimes applied here to a new domain. **Synthesis** identifies this book's reusable formulation or proposed application, without claiming exclusive invention. **Wager** identifies an exploratory extension whose value still needs testing. The imperative wording expresses a design preference under stated conditions, not an exceptionless law. Modularity, for example, earns its boundary through the changes it enables; it can also add joints, latency and maintenance.

The sources and worked examples live in the linked manuscripts and their footnotes. This is an editorial inventory, not a proposed twenty-four-part catalogue to insert into the book. Names can become more personal, witty or motivational in Sam's later prose while retaining the reasoning underneath. Values such as care and freedom remain essential purposes; the entries below explain structural choices that could serve them.

## Θ Foundations

### [Chapter Θ — The Architect’s Pause](../manuscripts/volume-3/theta-chapter.md)

- **Define the needed change before selecting the solution.** *Established: business analysis and requirements elicitation.* Describe current conditions, affected parties and desired capabilities; a preferred product is one candidate response. **Home:** “From a Principle to a Design.”
- **Decompose functions before allocating them.** *Established: functional decomposition and allocation.* Identify the necessary work, interfaces and responsibilities before deciding which institution, person or machine should perform each part.
- **Compare credible alternatives under explicit constraints.** *Established: decision and tradeoff analysis.* Include a simpler change or retained arrangement; expose assumptions, conflicts and sensitivity rather than let one weighted score settle every value.
- **Turn a principle into an observable scenario.** *Established: quality-attribute scenarios and traceability.* Connect a need to a design decision, a demanding operating condition, an expected response and a way to evaluate it. Keep verification of specification distinct from validation of usefulness.
- **Reopen the governing purpose before optimizing the machinery.** *Established footing: double-loop learning; book application.* Question the objective or category when efficient performance preserves the problem. **Added home:** “Change What Counts as Success.”
- **Connect cognition without collectivizing authority.** *Synthesis.* Integrate sensing, interpretation and inquiry while keeping the powers to act distinct, legitimate and contestable. **Strengthened home:** “The Missing Organ.”
- **Design conditions for emergence, not a compulsory outcome.** *Synthesis.* Build useful relationships and capacities, then examine what they enable together. The Brain–Neusphere scaling is a productive conjecture, not an organizational law.
- **Refactor the function while carrying its obligations forward.** *Synthesis.* Preserve what an inherited institution sustains while changing its purpose, form or implementation. Include the enclosing ecosystem in the design boundary. **Strengthened home:** “The Refactoring of Old Words.”

### [Interlude Θ — The Architect’s Bench](../manuscripts/volume-3/theta-interlude.md)

- **Build one complete loop before claiming a civilization.** *Synthesis; end-to-end integration practice.* Carry need through commitment, resources, delivery, assessment and correction. A demonstrable fragment must include the return path.
- **Increase exposure as the evidence earns it.** *Established experimental discipline, applied to the Breadboard.* Synthetic exercises, willing bounded pilots and sustained operation answer different questions. State what each result actually supports.
- **Count the support hidden outside the prototype.** *Synthesis.* Record founder time, subsidies, unpaid rescue and external services. A working integration is different from a self-sustaining economy; the pizza threshold belongs to 2034.
- **Design failure exercises before participants depend on success.** *Established testing practice.* Rehearse withdrawal, duplicates and loss of connection; preserve actual service continuity during live work rather than manufacture harm for the demonstration.
- **Make the experiment itself contestable.** *Synthesis.* Give participants a way to challenge measures, purposes and continuation; retain dissent alongside favorable outcomes. A green dashboard cannot ratify its own test.
- **Make deployment repeatable and recovery demonstrable.** *Established release engineering.* Version inputs, promote identifiable artifacts, keep configuration inspectable and rehearse restoration by another builder. **Added home:** “Build It Again Without Its Inventor.”
- **Recreate machinery without replaying consequences.** *Synthesis.* A recorded run can be examined without reissuing its real commands. Specify behavioral tolerances when exact computational repetition is unavailable.

## 23 Renewal

### [Chapter 23 — The Loom of Renewal](../manuscripts/volume-3/23-chapter.md)

- **Allocate the whole journey, including work between organizations.** *Established: functional allocation and service blueprinting.* Compare the participant's experience with backstage work, waits and responsibility gaps. **Home:** “From Institutions to Flows.”
- **Place module boundaries around consequential change.** *Established: information hiding.* Encapsulate decisions likely to vary, so a change of provider or implementation need not break every surrounding commitment. Modularity is a reasoning choice, not a command to split everything into services.
- **Compose capability around the need; let institutional shape follow.** *Synthesis.* Search for a different arrangement of learning, care, provision or place, including one that removes a handoff. **Added home:** “Let the Need Change the Arrangement.”
- **Separate coordination from provision.** *Synthesis.* Finding a route does not supply skill, stock, time or authority. Allocate these explicitly and give an unmet need an accountable response.
- **Make availability legible without conscripting it.** *Synthesis.* A spare room, skill or hour is not automatically an offer. Represent willingness and limits alongside capacity; discovering a possible match does not make a commitment.
- **Give each accepted promise an owner through the handoff.** *Synthesis.* Responsibility continues until legitimately transferred or fulfilled. A person should not have to reconstruct the organizational map to obtain help.
- **Let successful coordination reduce dependence on its coordinator.** *Wager.* Shared learning might establish direct, dependable relationships and retire unnecessary routing. Judge this by sustained capability and remaining duties, not by fewer visible administrators alone.

### [Interlude 23 — The Loom of Design](../manuscripts/volume-3/23-interlude.md)

- **Give distinct claims distinct states.** *Established modelling discipline.* Separate need, offer and commitment; define valid transitions, responsible actors and evidence of completion. A state machine represents selected obligations, not a whole life.
- **Keep domain operations behind stable ports.** *Established: Ports and Adapters.* Replace an interface or provider without rewriting the promise. Test adapters against the same external obligations.
- **Put terms on the relationship.** *Synthesis informed by domain modelling.* Carry purpose, direction, scope, authority, duration and change rules on an agreement; distinguish inferred relations from accepted obligations. **Added home:** “Make the Relationship Mean Something.”
- **Translate meanings, not only fields.** *Established: bounded contexts and explicit mappings.* A provider's “completed” need not mean the shared promise is fulfilled. Preserve important differences; governance later deepens this into jurisdictional translation.
- **Make retries refer to the same intention.** *Established: Idempotent Receiver.* Bind a stable operation identity to the accepted effect and stored result. Treat a lost response as uncertainty until reconciled; do not promise exactly-once physical action.
- **Bound the work generated by failure.** *Established retry discipline; agentic extension.* Use deadlines, backoff, retry budgets and bounded search/delegation. **Added home:** “Do Not Make Recovery the Next Failure.”
- **Place authority checks where work can actually happen.** *Established access-control principle.* Separate roles and relevant permissions; protect alternative routes, background actions and retries as well as the conversational interface.
- **Test the promise through adverse conditions.** *Established contract/integration testing.* Verify completion, cancellation, lost messages, provider withdrawal and fallback from the participant's side. A successful process exit is not sufficient evidence of useful help.

## 24 Agency

### [Chapter 24 — The Citizen’s Dilemma](../manuscripts/volume-3/24-chapter.md)

- **Translate values into choices people can actually exercise.** *Established: value-sensitive and human-centred design.* Investigate who is affected, what control costs them and whether an accessible alternative exists.
- **Delegate useful authority within a revisable envelope.** *Established least-privilege footing; civic application.* Permit substantial assistance without conferring general custody. Separate consequential powers where their combination would be dangerous.
- **Compose permissions and obligations across affected lives.** *Synthesis.* Joint plans should enlarge practical capability without transferring one person's workload to another by implication. **Added home:** “When Two Freedoms Share a Plan.”
- **Keep observations, inferences and decisions distinguishable.** *Established modelling discipline.* Correcting a profile must reach the consequential uses of that profile; a true observation can support a false inference.
- **Give representations a lifecycle suited to what they represent.** *Synthesis.* Volatile preferences, lasting qualifications and disputed history require different revision/retention rules. People must be able to outgrow a model without erasing legitimate obligations.
- **Preserve contextual selves rather than build one unrestricted dossier.** *Synthesis.* Participation across care, work, learning and culture need not expose every context to every other. Opacity can be part of agency.
- **Design usable recourse and exit alongside assistance.** *Established human–AI interaction principles; broader civic application.* A person can narrow, correct, replace or leave help without unrelated penalties or an impossible administrative burden.
- **Leave room for a self the model did not predict.** *Wager.* Assistance should help a person pursue a new direction, not only optimize the history from which her model was inferred.

### [Interlude 24 — The Developer’s Dilemma](../manuscripts/volume-3/24-interlude.md)

- **Separate preferences, grants and action receipts.** *Synthesis.* What a person likes, what an actor may do and what it actually did need distinct records and update rules.
- **Mediate every consequential execution route.** *Established: policy decision/enforcement separation, complete mediation and fail-safe defaults.* Bind the approved action to current authority; a queued proposal must not preserve permission indefinitely.
- **Attenuate delegated authority.** *Established security principle, with explicit civic scope.* Further delegation may preserve or narrow the relevant grant; a backend credential must not lend unrelated institutional powers to a caller.
- **Separate plan, authorization and execution state.** *Synthesis using service composition.* Replan the affected portion after change, preserve valid commitments and seek fresh authority for expanded effects. **Added home:** “Assemble the Help, Not Another App.”
- **Let initiative move without hiding control.** *Established: mixed-initiative interaction.* Useful automation, direct action and focused questions share the work. Consider uncertain intent and interruption costs; background operation still needs inspection and intervention routes.
- **Vary strategy without moving the permission boundary.** *Established: Strategy pattern.* Alternative scheduling or recommendation procedures share a contract; a more capable model does not automatically inherit a larger mandate.
- **Make revocation and correction propagate.** *Established lifecycle/reconciliation practices.* Stop future acts, examine queued work and resolve completed consequences separately. Expiry and cache behavior matter as much as the visible consent screen.
- **Test refusal and handover as ordinary use.** *Synthesis of security and usability testing.* Check that declined suggestions do not create hidden penalties, and that a replacement helper receives usable records and duties without inheriting unauthorized credentials.

## 25 Economy

### [Chapter 25 — Beyond the Invisible Hand](../manuscripts/volume-3/25-chapter.md)

- **Design a route from productive gain to usable benefit.** *Established entitlement analysis; design synthesis.* Distinguish greater output from the claims that let someone obtain provision, time or security. Compare cash, services, ownership and contribution-linked arrangements.
- **Test the rule against strategic and ordinary behavior.** *Established: mechanism design and incentive analysis.* A reward may select easy work, exaggerated urgency or measured activity rather than useful outcomes. Goodwill is also affected by the rules around it.
- **Keep constraints outside compensatory scoring.** *Established multi-objective design.* First identify feasible arrangements, then compare tradeoffs and Pareto dominance. A weighted benefit cannot silently purchase its way through every protected boundary.
- **Separate recognition from entitlement to necessities.** *Synthesis.* Record contribution without making essential provision depend on performing measurable virtue. Identify the provider and funding of the provision floor.
- **Distinguish circulation, reserve and hoarding.** *Synthesis.* Inspect purpose, future need and renewal obligations before treating an idle resource as waste. Anti-hoarding incentives can themselves create unnecessary activity.
- **Design finance around the capacity it can bring into being.** *Synthesis.* Recurring unmet needs can inform investment and provision, rather than merely improve the ranking of rejected requests. Keep investment risk and external funding visible.
- **Compare whole flows under changed conditions.** *Established stock/flow and sensitivity analysis.* Vary demand, supply, productivity gains and provider failure; inspect who bears the shortfall and which essential obligations survive.
- **Use abundance to enlarge unmeasured life.** *Wager.* A dependable floor might permit more private care, experimentation and freedom from compulsory legibility. Test who performs the work that remains.

### [Interlude 25 — The Living Ledger](../manuscripts/volume-3/25-interlude.md)

- **Separate evidence, assessment, entitlement, reservation, delivery and settlement.** *Established modelling/accounting discipline applied here.* Related records establish different things; recognition does not become spendable value by being placed in a graph.
- **Use the graph shape the relation requires.** *Established graph modelling.* Economic circulation can cycle; a selected derivation history may be a DAG. Neither structure establishes causation, consensus or valid attribution.
- **Enforce resource invariants at the actual reservation boundary.** *Established transaction/concurrency practice.* Concurrent requests cannot independently spend the same residual capacity. Scope identifiers, constraints and locks to the business rule.
- **Commit state and its outgoing notification together.** *Established: Transactional Outbox.* A local update and the message announcing it share a transaction; subsequent delivery needs duplicate handling and reconciliation.
- **Compensate across services without pretending time reversed.** *Established: Saga/compensating transactions.* Release unused reservations or reverse an authorization where appropriate; consumed goods and real harms need remedies that rollback cannot supply.
- **Let temporary routes retire while durable consequences remain.** *Synthesis.* A provision workflow can dissolve after fulfillment; unpaid claims, warranties and justified learning records survive under their own retention rules.
- **Audit different views without exposing the whole person.** *Synthesis.* Public condition, individual case and independent review need different access. Reconcile against actual resources and outside settlement, including failed and abandoned requests.
- **Make boundary value an accountable obligation.** *Synthesis.* Conversion, redemption, liquidity and loss allocation require named terms and funding. The pizza test cannot be passed by obscuring a founder subsidy or inventing an exchange guarantee.
- **Let the ledger question its missing relations.** *Wager.* Propose omitted dependencies from discrepancies, then investigate them before creating rewards or obligations. A plausible edge is not evidence that someone earned a claim.

## 26 Learning and shared reality

### [Chapter 26 — Minds in Harmony](../manuscripts/volume-3/26-chapter.md)

- **Model what a statement is doing.** *Established: concept modelling.* Distinguish observation, inference, prediction, recommendation and value judgment; connect each to the kind of challenge that could change it.
- **Align learning aims, activities and assessment.** *Established: constructive alignment.* If the aim is judgment or transfer, a fluent answer generated by the tutor cannot demonstrate the learner acquired it.
- **Fade pedagogical scaffolding without removing necessary access.** *Established: cognitive apprenticeship.* Evaluate independent understanding and effective assisted practice separately; permanent aids can enlarge capability without making learning fraudulent.
- **Maintain knowledge as a lifecycle.** *Synthesis.* Link inquiry, publication, learning and correction so an improvement can return to the places where the old account matters. An informational commons is a resource for Neusphere, not its synonym.
- **Separate evidence disputes from conflicts of value and authority.** *Established value-sensitive design; plural-order application.* Use evidence and order maps together. A better measurement cannot authorize sacrificing someone else's place.
- **Evaluate a warning against a defined event and decision.** *Established forecast/evaluation discipline.* Record horizon, uncertainty, missed events and false alarms; urgency and certainty need separate treatment.
- **Design attention around understanding and useful action.** *Synthesis.* Evaluate comprehension, correction and discovery rather than engagement alone. Quiet and exit may be successful outcomes.
- **Make the timing of integration a design variable.** *Wager.* Preserve useful independent accounts before synthesis; reconnect them when comparison can expose errors or new possibilities. More disagreement alone is not the goal.

### [Interlude 26 — Signal and Story](../manuscripts/volume-3/26-interlude.md)

- **Give claims and their support distinct addresses.** *Established: typed provenance and argument models.* Preserve source versions, evidence relationships and permitted access; authentic origin is not proof that a claim is established.
- **Make processing stages independently examinable.** *Established: modular pipelines and Ports/Adapters.* Separate retrieval, assessment, inference, rendering and publication. Evaluate actual source support rather than merely the presence of a citation.
- **Keep rival hypotheses in a working memory.** *Established: blackboard architecture; new civic application.* Specialists contribute partial interpretations and choose useful next inquiry without collapsing every view into one early answer. **Added home:** “Give Inquiry a Working Memory.”
- **Carry corrections through dependencies and consequences.** *Established publish–subscribe/idempotency; civic synthesis.* Version the claim, locate known uses, request review and track unresolved effects. Updating a source does not retrain every model or undo a decision.
- **Use the right evaluation for the claimed achievement.** *Established.* Separate source accuracy, calibration, model performance, learning, transfer and experience. A Brier score evaluates defined forecasts, not the moral status of a river.
- **Make experiments inspectable across variable runs.** *Established reproducibility practice.* Retain relevant versions, cases, configuration and rubrics; report what can actually be repeated, including hosted-model limitations.
- **Keep recommendation policy replaceable.** *Established: Strategy.* Offer alternative orderings under the same access contract and inspect what each ordering makes visible, rather than let a feed secretly become the record.
- **Search for hidden dependence before counting corroboration.** *Wager.* Detect likely shared ancestors of apparently independent accounts and select evidence that would discriminate between explanations. Investigate uncertain lineage rather than promote suspicion to fact.

## 27 Commons

### [Chapter 27 — The Reclaimed Commons](../manuscripts/volume-3/27-chapter.md)

- **Separate the resource from the rights regime and its enforcement.** *Established institutional analysis.* Water, compute, code and cultural practice have different use effects; one open-access slogan cannot govern all four.
- **Design use and upkeep together.** *Established Ostrom principles; domain-specific application.* Fit boundaries, rules, monitoring, revision, conflict resolution and nested responsibilities to the particular commons. Shared access does not perform maintenance.
- **Fit the part within the ecosystem that sustains it.** *Established ecosystem-approach footing; book formulation.* Widen the boundary to cumulative effects, other users, nonhuman life and recovery times. Human benefit alone is an incomplete objective.
- **Protect non-substitutable limits before ranking options.** *Synthesis.* Carbon savings do not automatically replace habitat, and money cannot reverse every loss. Keep resource accounts in meaningful units and scales.
- **Tie use to observed condition and a response.** *Established adaptive-management discipline.* Define conservative envelopes, warning thresholds, accountable revision and recovery evidence. An accounting anniversary does not replenish a slow ecosystem.
- **Give local knowledge and affected parties practical standing.** *Established commons governance, with specific sovereignty obligations.* Participation must alter choices; a generic “community” label cannot represent every person or Nation.
- **Sustain the means to leave or replace the steward.** *Synthesis.* Maintain access, operating knowledge, resources and succession so dependence on a common service does not become dependence on its current host.
- **Let increasing intelligence improve the capacity to belong.** *Wager.* Couple growing privileges to independently observed recovery; compare this with treating ecological information as advice while rewarding throughput.

### [Interlude 27 — Streams and Sanctuaries](../manuscripts/volume-3/27-interlude.md)

- **Keep resource condition, grant of use and actual use distinct.** *Established modelling discipline.* An authorized query does not establish physical capacity, nor does an observation authorize an intervention.
- **Discover legitimate data authority before collecting.** *Established OCAP/CARE frameworks in their proper scopes.* Individual permission, collective authority and legal duties are related but non-interchangeable; some knowledge is not shareable.
- **Enforce limits at controlled operations and outputs.** *Established ABAC and policy enforcement.* Purpose labels alone cannot control every future use. Identify where technical controls end and contractual or institutional obligations begin.
- **Admit work against cumulative budgets and reserves.** *Established admission control; ecological extension.* Reserve atomically or allocate safe local quotas, account for existing commitments and reconcile actual consumption. Backpressure cannot create missing water.
- **Choose a privacy guarantee for a specified threat.** *Established privacy engineering.* Differential privacy, secure computation, zero-knowledge proofs and federated learning protect different things. Account for composition and what outputs reveal.
- **Separate payload, authority and accountability lifetimes.** *Synthesis.* Data retention, credential expiry and continuing duties need different clocks. Preserve justified evidence without creating permanent personal exposure.
- **Test continuity and limits together.** *Established resilience testing.* Exercise stale observations, concurrent claims, revoked grants, failed custody and capacity loss; refusal must not silently abandon essential commitments.
- **Make obligations survive a change of custodian.** *Wager.* Translate a purpose covenant through new data and service arrangements while preserving the materially relevant duty; expose failed translation rather than acknowledge it ceremonially.

## 28 Making

### [Chapter 28 — The Reimagined Workshop](../manuscripts/volume-3/28-chapter.md)

- **Specify the useful function before ordering the object.** *Established functional analysis.* Compare repair, substitution, changed conditions and new manufacture against the same need. Faster fabrication is not automatically better provision.
- **Choose interfaces for the changes the object must survive.** *Established product architecture and modularity.* Design assembly, servicing and disassembly together; assess the costs of joints, seals and variation instead of declaring modularity universally superior.
- **Compare whole lives on a common functional unit.** *Established life-cycle assessment.* Include yield, operation, maintenance, actual lifetime and recovery; expand the function fairly when a whole-system replacement is compared with a part repair.
- **Measure total pressure as well as efficiency per item.** *Established systems/lifecycle reasoning.* Better unit performance can be overwhelmed by more production. Preserve ecological limits outside compensatory scoring.
- **Let material memory serve stewardship rather than surveillance.** *Synthesis.* Composition, hazards, repair and lineage should travel with the object; its user's intimate biography need not.
- **Turn failure analysis into a design change.** *Established FMEA and systems reasoning.* Examine consequences and interactions, remove dangerous paths where possible and distinguish design/process qualification from acceptance of one output.
- **Make responsibility continue after delivery.** *Synthesis.* Fund maintenance, repair, recall and recovery; a workshop is a continuing capability rather than a printer attached to a mission statement.
- **Grow capability faster than material replacement.** *Wager.* Share lifecycle learning across objects and workshops so existing stock can acquire new usefulness. Test for rebound and the preservation of harmful old arrangements.

### [Interlude 28 — Neufacturing](../manuscripts/volume-3/28-interlude.md)

- **Keep design, route, instance and evidence separate.** *Established configuration/quality practice.* A validated geometry is not an accepted physical part; each instance carries the process and material history relevant to its use.
- **Search within a manufacturable, testable envelope.** *Established parametric design, topology optimization and process qualification.* Generated forms must also be inspectable, maintainable and consistently producible.
- **Adapt interfaces; construct compatible component families.** *Established Adapter and Abstract Factory patterns.* The first translates an interface; the second assembles related software implementations for a production route. Neither certifies the material output.
- **Design fit across ranges, then monitor the process.** *Established tolerance analysis and statistical process control.* Include measurement uncertainty and relevant conditions. Control limits and specification limits answer different questions.
- **Preserve transformation lineage without a compulsory owner dossier.** *Established event/traceability practice.* Link batches, designs, parts, repairs and recovery; distinguish passport, lifecycle record and digital twin.
- **Keep consequential actuation within local operating limits.** *Established operational-technology safety practice; TACK as book framing.* Separate open-ended planning from physical execution, interlocks and process-specific safe interruption.
- **Reconcile uncertain effects before issuing another physical command.** *Established distributed/physical-systems practice.* A lost acknowledgment does not prove that a cut or movement did not occur. Recovery may require inspection of the workpiece.
- **Let evidence travel with a changed design.** *Wager.* Reuse justified evidence across variants while identifying what the change invalidates and what needs new qualification. Similar appearance alone cannot carry approval.

## 29 Governance

### [Chapter 29 — The Steward’s Edge](../manuscripts/volume-3/29-chapter.md)

- **Place authority at the scale of the consequence.** *Established subsidiarity and polycentric governance.* Keep decisions close to relevant knowledge while addressing effects that cross the local boundary.
- **Make the intervention proportionate and revisable.** *Established proportionality.* Powers need purposes, scope and review; technical capability is not an entitlement to govern.
- **Design participation that people can afford.** *Synthesis.* Time, access, information and support determine whether formal standing becomes usable influence. Participation must not become compulsory civic homework.
- **Delegate without making representation permanent possession.** *Synthesis.* Keep scope, duration, amendment and recall intelligible, with continuity when representation changes.
- **Keep political membership distinct from authentication.** *Established distinction; civic design rule.* A credential can support recognition without determining citizenship, rights or the legitimacy of a collective decision.
- **Follow who bears a levy rather than whom it names.** *Established tax-incidence analysis.* A machine-oriented fiscal proposal needs a defensible base, enforceability and attention to shifted burdens; a clever tax title cannot fund provision.
- **Build institutions that can be corrected in operation.** *Synthesis.* Make rule changes, review, evidence and implementation reachable; responsibility needs an address through a distributed chain.
- **Use intelligence to reduce dependence on unaccountable centres.** *Wager.* Test whether stronger shared cognition enlarges distributed agency or merely hides a new central dependency.

### [Interlude 29 — Civic Meshes](../manuscripts/volume-3/29-interlude.md)

- **Distinguish person, credential, role and authority.** *Established identity/authorization modelling.* Authentication establishes a bounded claim; mandates supply the permitted action and its legitimate source.
- **Preserve institutional meanings across integrations.** *Established Bounded Context and Anti-Corruption Layer.* Map shared operations without allowing a receiving model to overwrite jurisdiction or obligation.
- **Attach an inspectable mandate to consequential action.** *Synthesis using established identity/security mechanisms.* Record source, actor, scope, context, duration, version and challenge route; keep sensitive evidence appropriately restricted.
- **Constrain every step of delegated authority.** *Established attenuation.* Preserve or narrow permitted scope along a chain; prevent technical replication from multiplying civic standing.
- **Version policy as policy.** *Established decision modelling, including DMN.* Separate changing rules and their authority from software deployment; formalization cannot supply legitimacy by itself.
- **Choose partition behavior per operation.** *Established distributed-systems reasoning.* A network split forces choices about availability and consistency; time-bounded leases and reconciliation need explicit failure assumptions. No theorem chooses the civic priority.
- **Migrate incrementally while preserving obligations.** *Established Strangler Fig application replacement.* Route selected responsibilities to a successor, compare outcomes and retain an accountable transition rather than replace the constitution with the implementation.
- **Test what a rule loses in translation.** *Wager.* A translation dossier should expose semantic differences, disputed implications and the cases that would reveal a consequential mismatch.

## 30 Justice

### [Chapter 30 — Justice Before Judgment](../manuscripts/volume-3/30-chapter.md)

- **Design the conditions of choice before judging its outcome.** *Synthesis informed by capabilities and systems analysis.* Change deprivation, coercion, unsafe arrangements and inaccessible routes; responsibility for actual choices still matters.
- **Test the rule against the capability it is meant to protect.** *Established decision modelling; civilizational QA extension.* A consistent eligibility rule can consistently frustrate its purpose. The specification belongs within the scope of review.
- **Analyze control relationships, not only faulty components.** *Established STPA footing.* Harm can arise from correct local actions combined at the wrong time or under the wrong assumptions.
- **Follow distributed causes while keeping duties assignable.** *Synthesis.* Causal complexity need not erase accountability, and one responsible actor need not become the explanation of the whole failure.
- **Design protection and investigation on different clocks.** *Synthesis.* Urgent continuity may precede a final finding; provisional protection must not quietly become irreversible punishment.
- **Repair the class of injury, including people who never complained.** *Synthesis.* Correct the recurring mechanism and downstream consequences; keep access, missing records and silent exclusion in the evaluation.
- **Prevent harm without converting prediction into guilt.** *Established rights/proportionality footing.* Compare less restrictive adequate measures and their actual effects. A risk score is not a verdict.
- **Let justice revise its own categories and questions.** *Wager.* Cross-domain learning may reveal that a classification repeatedly creates avoidable dependency, then generate a better form of provision rather than optimize rejection.

### [Interlude 30 — Protocols of Integrity](../manuscripts/volume-3/30-interlude.md)

- **Build an assurance case that can be defeated.** *Established claims–argument–evidence practice.* State context, assumptions and counterevidence; an attractive diagram is not proof of fitness for use.
- **Make action fitness operational.** *Synthesis: TACK.* Translate Tactical, Aligned, Constrained and Knowable into purpose, legitimate values, operating limits and inspectable effects. TACK is not a certification.
- **Analyze the control loop and specify contracts across its seams.** *Established STPA and Design by Contract.* Identify unsafe actions, missing feedback, preconditions, postconditions and invariants; include authority and meaning, not merely valid data types.
- **Generate difficult cases and test justified relations.** *Established property-based and metamorphic testing.* When a full answer oracle is unavailable, test relevant invariants and relationships between cases; record falsifying examples for regression.
- **Release a tested configuration and carry its obligations through change.** *Established CI/CD and migration practice.* Version code, models, policy and transformations; rehearse migration/recovery. **Strengthened home:** “Release the Change, Keep the Obligation.”
- **Preserve evidence of effects rather than fluent explanations of intent.** *Established observability/provenance practice.* Separate observations, inferences, commands and outcomes; distinguish event time from receipt time and scope retention.
- **Operate protection, investigation and remedy as connected work.** *Synthesis.* A patch must reach affected services and lives. Use idempotent correction, escalation and funded repair rather than equate acknowledgment with resolution.
- **Give the integrity machinery its own review.** *Synthesis.* Audit collection, burdens, missing cases and complaint access. Integrity controls can themselves create harm.
- **Make repair propagate as effectively as failure.** *Wager.* A repair protocol carries reasons, scope, authority, continuity and outstanding consequences across unlike systems; failed translation remains an unresolved duty.

## 31 Care and medicine

### [Chapter 31 — The Flow of Care](../manuscripts/volume-3/31-chapter.md)

- **Intervene at the causal relationship sustaining the problem.** *Systems/network-medicine footing; central book formulation.* Disease labels remain useful summaries, while coupled pathways, exposures and treatment effects determine the intervention problem.
- **Design around what life could regain.** *Established goal-oriented requirements and minimally disruptive medicine.* Link physiological improvement to meaningful outcomes and the work imposed by treatment; gracious service cannot substitute for effective intervention.
- **Let the team follow the changing need.** *Established functional allocation and SEIPS.* Allocate tasks among people, agents, facilities and tools according to capacity, evidence and context rather than departmental permanence.
- **Treat interventions as part of the system being treated.** *Established pharmacological/systems reasoning; Rx-ome as author term.* Include actual use, interactions, exposure and feedback across scales. Correlation edges must earn causal interpretations.
- **Preserve future options under uncertain response.** *Established robust/adaptive design.* Compare immediate benefit, reversibility, future treatment capacity and changing biology rather than optimize a single present measurement.
- **Allow treatment to follow an evolving target.** *Established evolutionary-therapy footing; future extension.* Disease and therapy can change one another. The design needs observation and revision, not a permanent victory declaration after one response.
- **Compose care beyond the screen and the clinic.** *Established service design; post-app application.* Join biological intervention, physical assistance, rehabilitation, environment and provision without making the person the integration engine.
- **Keep health inside its enclosing social and ecological systems.** *Established boundary-setting principle.* Improved care must account for exposures, upstream conditions, material use and displaced burdens.
- **Let a causal map propose what the diagnostic drawers cannot.** *Wager.* Generate and test new intervention possibilities while distinguishing explanation, evidence and clinical authority. VitalEdge explores; the broader future medical system may diagnose and treat.

### [Interlude 31 — From Molecule to Moment](../manuscripts/volume-3/31-interlude.md)

- **Integrate biological scales without erasing provenance or time.** *Established multimodal modelling.* Relate omics, physiology, imaging and lived observations; measurements from different moments or compartments are not interchangeable features.
- **Model exposure as well as nominal dose.** *Established PK/PD and physiologically based modelling.* Delivery, clearance and interaction shape effects. Use explicit assumptions and uncertainty rather than let a medication list stand in for the treatment environment.
- **Carry an intervention through its entire evidence chain.** *Established translational/platform discipline.* Target validity, delivery, exposure, effect and tolerability need connected evidence. A generated molecule or edited sequence is an intermediate result.
- **Qualify the process as well as the product.** *Established biotechnology and tissue-engineering practice.* Gene/cell manufacture, tissue maturation, vascularization and functional performance impose distinct constraints; printing is not the whole graft.
- **Close the learning loop around actual outcomes.** *Established adaptive clinical-development principles.* Monitor response and changing disease, then revise within an authorized pathway. Retrospective accuracy, shadow operation and clinical studies support different claims.
- **Spend inquiry where it can change the treatment decision.** *Established value-of-information reasoning.* Digital twins and surrogate models should identify consequential uncertainty and useful next measurements, not merely supply more realistic pictures.
- **Assemble specialist capability around a shared case.** *Established blackboard, capability registry, Adapter and event-driven coordination patterns.* Keep observation, hypothesis, decision, action and outcome distinct. The pattern introduced in I26 becomes clinical here.
- **Complete the intervention through follow-up and recovery.** *Established workflow orchestration and physical-effects discipline.* Do not duplicate a dose or procedure after a lost reply; cancellation and changes must reach every actor. A software rollback cannot undo implanted tissue.
- **Explore the therapeutic design space beyond inherited labels.** *Wager.* Join mechanistic hypotheses, generative tools and experiments to search for useful interventions whose eventual clinical status still has to be established.

## 32 Continuity

### [Chapter 32 — Threads of Continuity](../manuscripts/volume-3/32-chapter.md)

- **Preserve the capacity an inheritance sustains, not every frozen expression.** *Synthesis.* Distinguish language, practice, identity, evidence and authority; transmission can legitimately include revision.
- **Make inherited commitments do useful work.** *Synthesis.* Reinterpret retained words through present obligations and consequences. Compatibility with science cannot be obtained by declaring every literal claim empirically true.
- **Renew with the people who carry the tradition.** *Established participatory/value-sensitive design footing.* Keep internal disagreement and interpretive authority visible. Neither a central curator nor an AI speaks for all believers or Indigenous peoples.
- **Integrate through shared practice without requiring a common creed.** *Synthesis: MOxAIC application.* Joint work can change participants while preserving meaningful differences. Concept modelling should retain distinctions such as ownership and custodianship.
- **Track how benefits and burdens cross memberships.** *Established distributional design reasoning.* Equal speaking time can hide unequal labor or exposure; overlapping belonging must not multiply claims to the same resource.
- **Evaluate commitments in action under pressure.** *Synthesis.* Test costly help, new evidence, outside beneficiaries and ecological consequences. Reciting compassion is not the same achievement as organizing it.
- **Preserve room for the unrecorded.** *Synthesis.* Ritual, art, silence and living transmission do not all need a digital surrogate. A richer archive can still damage what it is meant to sustain.
- **Inherit the ability to correct the inheritance.** *Wager.* Distinct traditions and participating agents might enlarge collective judgment through reciprocal correction, rather than converge on a flattened moral vocabulary.

### [Interlude 32 — Encoding the Eternal](../manuscripts/volume-3/32-interlude.md)

- **Preserve technical and interpretive continuity separately.** *Established digital-preservation discipline.* Retaining bits and renderability does not guarantee that a future reader understands the context or has legitimate access.
- **Give source, interpretation and revision separate identities.** *Established provenance/versioning practice.* Record edition, language, translator, context and disputed readings without inserting later agreements into ancestral mouths.
- **Translate with an account of what the mapping loses.** *Established semantic mapping and Adapter practice.* Close matches are not exact equivalences or freely transitive relations. Preserve unresolved obligations and responsible interpreters.
- **Trace a value from source to consequence.** *Synthesis.* Keep passage, interpretation, commitment, decision, action, outcome and revision linked but distinct. Policy languages express selected rules; they cannot derive a just interpretation by themselves.
- **Compose plural priorities before reducing them to a score.** *Established multi-objective reasoning; MOxAIC application.* Show the distribution of effects and search for better options. Pareto improvement is useful but not a certificate of fairness.
- **Separate learned disposition, retrieved guidance and enforced authority.** *Established AI-system architecture distinction.* Training, current context and runtime controls do different jobs. Evaluate behavior in unfamiliar and inconvenient cases.
- **Preserve a living realm without equating its model with the person.** *Synthesis.* A reflective personal system needs bounded access, consent and uncertainty; synthetic completion must not erase meaningful gaps.
- **Migrate the lineage of correction, not only the final rule.** *Wager.* Transfer why commitments changed and whom they failed, then test a successor under new capabilities. There is no checksum for moral continuity.

## 33 Reality and release

### [Chapter 33 — The Arrow’s Release](../manuscripts/volume-3/33-chapter.md)

- **Give the design an answer that could defeat it.** *Established hypothesis-driven design.* Compare with alternatives and actual consequences; implementation success is not evidence that the intervention helps.
- **Distinguish physical constraints from chosen obligations.** *Established constraint modelling; book application.* Both matter, but they are challenged and revised through different processes. An optimizer cannot give itself permission to change either.
- **Choose arrangements that survive several plausible futures.** *Established robust decision-making.* Inspect vulnerabilities and adaptive capacity rather than crown the winner of one forecast. Spare capacity and flexibility have costs that must earn their place.
- **Investigate the uncertainty that can alter the decision.** *Established value of information.* More detail is not automatically more useful; match inquiry to the next consequential choice while retaining room for open exploration.
- **Let constructed worlds enlarge real capability.** *Synthesis.* Evaluate access, transfer, inquiry and relationships beyond the representation; art and experience can matter without pretending to be empirical proof.
- **Design for replacement, retirement and another steward.** *Established lifecycle/transition practice; founder-release application.* Carry assets, operating knowledge and obligations across change. Founder absence should not disable legitimate revision.
- **Keep mortality and representation distinct without foreclosing inquiry.** *Philosophical design boundary.* Resemblance, continued agency and subjective continuity are separate questions; none should be silently promised by preservation software.
- **Search for new forms of life, not only better parameters.** *Wager.* Change the units of the civic model—how care, learning, making and belonging combine—and test what becomes feasible beyond inherited institutional categories.

### [Interlude 33 — Machines as Mirrors](../manuscripts/volume-3/33-interlude.md)

- **Separate the physical substrate, represented world and consequences of use.** *Established modelling distinction; book formulation.* A simulation can change its own gravity while remaining dependent on real machines, materials and people.
- **Scope model credibility to the intended claim.** *Established verification, validation and uncertainty analysis.* Calibration, analytic checks, new observations and sensitivity answer different questions. Credibility does not transfer automatically between uses or coupled components.
- **Package the experiment so another group can challenge it.** *Established reproducibility practice.* Preserve relevant versions, conditions and criteria; state tolerances or statistical behavior when exact replay is unavailable.
- **Allocate model fidelity and measurement where they matter.** *Established multifidelity/surrogate approach with prospective extension.* Use cheap approximations for search, more detailed inquiry where warranted and measurements to challenge both.
- **Cross from rehearsal to action through distinct authority.** *Established staged evaluation and controlled integration.* Synthetic, recorded, shadow and live work have different permissions and evidentiary limits. A prompt flag is not a physical actuator boundary.
- **Test succession by removing the founder from the critical path.** *Synthesis.* Another group should recover, inspect, revise and retire an instance while continuing justified obligations. Inherit the means of inquiry, not just the executable.
- **Build the Mirror as a useful cognitive relationship.** *Extended-mind antecedent; proposed architecture.* Separate evidence, retrieval, personal modelling, rendering and delegation. Measure what person and system can do together, not merely resemblance to the person's voice.
- **Keep generated art and claimed evidence legible as different achievements.** *Synthesis.* Art need not masquerade as history, and an emotionally powerful encounter need not prove personal survival.
- **Investigate a successor's useful difference as well as its fidelity.** *Wager.* A Mirror or successor may help reveal contradictions and possibilities its source could not hold alone. Greater usefulness does not settle consciousness or identity.
