Keep the Chapter 14 hidden-cultures motivation active and re-read it before writing its return. The author's larger inquiry is whether AI can reduce compulsory drudgery and release people from inherited control arrangements, enabling richer relationships with one another and the wider ecosystem. Ideals are not the enemy; hardened mechanisms that turn people into their servants are. Neusphere is the civilizational aspiration, with technology helping make it possible. This should remain an exploration, not a product pitch or an assertion that foraging was universally ideal.

Editorial framing: recover rootedness, reciprocal obligation, freely chosen time and ecological competence, while respecting Indigenous peoples as distinct living communities with their own rights and futures. The author's phrase about becoming our own indigenous selves conveys belonging; public prose should avoid conflating that universal aspiration with possessing an Indigenous identity. Retiring coercive arrangements still requires workable provision for coordination, care, disagreement and accountability. Freedom from unnecessary machinery is compatible with consciously designed institutions.

## Targeted source checks made in this response

These add to the candidate table's source trail. They do not constitute a fresh audit of every previously researched anchor.

- **Nowruz:** Mary Boyce documents the problem of early attestation and its developed traditions; no defensible 750 BCE origin follows. Use a motif without a founding date. [Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/nowruz-i/).
- **Rome and Homer:** Rome's traditional 753 BCE foundation is explicitly legendary in the city's own account; the Homeric epics are broadly placed around the eighth century, not securely written in 750. [Rome](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/page/rome-nutshell); [Metropolitan Museum](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/greek-gods-and-religious-practices).
- **Cyrus to Darius:** Cyrus's conquests and Darius's consolidation support an opening with the mature empire. Use the existing specialist Ionian source for the revolt's 499 start; the museum overview is not the source for that exact date. [Metropolitan Museum](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/the-achaemenid-persian-empire-550-330-b-c); [Iranica: Ionian Revolt](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/ionian-revolt/).
- **Hamilton:** the 16 January 1801 Bayard letter argues for preferring Jefferson to Burr and explicitly fears Burr's attempted usurpation. It is a partisan contemporary judgment that can carry the author's analogy, not a neutral prophecy of 2026. [Founders Online, primary letter](https://founders.archives.gov/documents/Hamilton/01-25-02-0169).
- **1826:** the January 24 treaty's preamble records the challenge to the earlier signatories' authority; Article 1 voids the 1825 treaty and Article 2 cedes land. This supplies a concrete consent/value/land tension. [Treaty text, Oklahoma State University](https://treaties.okstate.edu/treaties/treaty-with-the-creeks-1826-0264).
- **1851:** the State Department records a treaty finalized that November which did not go into effect after a Senate-added commercial clause received no Persian action. Therefore an exact-year Iran aside is available without borrowing the later treaty. [Office of the Historian](https://history.state.gov/countries/iran).
- **1251:** Arghun Aqa's court visit and account of financial disorder belong to 1251–1252; the ensuing census/tax measures to 1253. Hulagu's Iranian campaign and Alamut's fall belong later, in 1256. Frame wounded populations and recovery as conditions; do not invent a 1251 massacre or exact casualty total. [Arghun Aqa](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/argun-aqa-a-mongol-administrator-in-iran-d-1275/); [Hulagu](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/hulagu-khan/).
- **1976:** the CDC retrospective distinguishes the limited Fort Dix outbreak, prospective pandemic risk, and suspension following the Guillain–Barré safety signal in the absence of spreading H1N1. This supports care under uncertainty, not a parallel mass-death epidemic. [CDC retrospective](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/12/1/05-1007_article).
- **2001:** UNESCO confirms the destruction of the Bamiyan statues in March. Its commemoration on March 11 does not itself establish a single destruction day for the whole episode. [UNESCO site record](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/208/); [commemoration](https://whc.unesco.org/en/news/2253).
