# Chapter 1 — Relational existence before physical ontology

**Author clarification and editorial follow-up · 4 October 2026.** Direction recorded; no manuscript revision or new physical theory established. Supplements the [physics review](../../volume-1-drafting/chapter-01-physics/CHAPTER_01_PHYSICS_REVIEW.md).

> Energy in this realization is not something that exists. Nothing "exists." Everything is relative conformational bias. The problem we are trying to tackle is that physicists assume a spacetime, or primordial big bang energy. We need to rethink that. And standard physics is just describing what it measures. If you have a mathematical fit that just means simplicity exists. Which leads to the question why is this structure or simplicity. We have to find a way to explain why. It's tricky. Unless we can reference some physicist who has figured that out. If not, then you and I have to onus of figuring that out. Using physics establish physics does not metaphorically move the arrow it just tells you how the arrow is moving in a circle.

## What this changes in the review

The author's target is the origin or necessity of relational structure and regularity themselves. Do not answer by supplying primordial energy, an already constituted spacetime, or an unexplained physical law. The earlier review's question about physical actualization must allow the possibility that existence is exhausted by relational structure; it must not require an additional material substance by definition. Whether such an account succeeds remains open.

Editorial reading, not an adopted formal definition: “nothing exists” here appears to deny independently self-subsisting things, while giving relational configurations and their biases explanatory priority. This differs from literal absence of every relation or distinction. Preserve the author's phrase “relative conformational bias”; its mathematical meaning is still to be worked out. Do not silently equate it with Boltzmann weights, quantum amplitudes, energy minimization, or algorithmic probability.

The factual corrections in the original review remain applicable wherever the chapter describes known physics. They do not settle the deeper ontology. Mathematical fit alone does not guarantee simplicity; economical prediction of independent observations is the stronger explanandum. Nor does a simple description by itself prove a simple underlying ontology.

## Proposed development work — pending, not a result

- State the minimum assumed structure: distinguishable configurations, relations, compatibility, or some other primitive. An empty physical inventory is not automatically an absence of mathematical assumptions.
- Determine what “bias” means and whether it can follow from that structure. Assuming that simple configurations are more favoured and deriving simplicity would leave the central question unanswered. Counting outcomes is different from weighting descriptions or generators.
- Treat search initially as a mathematical organization of alternatives if time is itself to emerge. Avoid importing a literal processor, chronological steps, physical computation costs, or an external searcher without acknowledging them.
- Examine whether records, recurrent relationships and internal perspectives could make persistent regularity intelligible. These are candidate ingredients, not a derivation; observer selection alone does not explain the particular regularity or its continuation.
- Require the proposed account eventually to recover the relevant measured behaviour, and identify where it differs from a reinterpretation of existing equations. Existing physics supplies constraints and successful limits, not the assumed substance of the explanation.

The task can develop a defensible foundational conjecture without pretending that a verbal restatement has solved the origin of law. A satisfactory next step would reduce the unexplained assumptions or establish a nontrivial consequence.

## Additional primary-source bookmarks

These supplement the existing 43-source dossier; its catalog and counts are unchanged. Links are research bookmarks, not endorsements or adopted manuscript citations.

1. **Markus P. Müller, “Law without law: from observer states to physics via algorithmic information theory,” Quantum 4, 301 (2020), DOI 10.22331/q-2020-07-20-301.** [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1712.01826v5). Checked introduction, postulates, probability rationale and conclusion. A close methodological precedent: apparent external regularity is derived within an observer-state/algorithmic-prior framework. Those starting assumptions remain; the paper explicitly describes its incompleteness. It does not establish the author's relational ontology or an assumption-free origin of physics.
2. **Jürgen Schmidhuber, “Algorithmic Theories of Everything,” IDSIA-20-00, version 2 (2000).** [Introduction](https://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/toesv2/node2.html). Rechecked the assumptions governing describable histories and their probability distribution. Useful for distinguishing an ensemble of possibilities from the measure that favours some of them. Mathematical results within that framework do not establish its cosmological premises. Already represented in the main dossier.
3. **Lee Smolin, “Unification of the state with the dynamical law” (2012), arXiv:1201.2632.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.2632). Abstract and author HTML consulted. A matrix model explores the distinction between laws and states as approximate and emergent. It retains evolution in time and therefore addresses part of the problem rather than an origin without temporal assumptions.
4. **Tomasz Konopka, Fotini Markopoulou and Simone Severini, “Quantum Graphity: a model of emergent locality,” Physical Review D 77, 104029 (2008).** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.0861). Abstract inspected. A counterexample to the blanket claim that all physicists assume fundamental spatial geometry. Its graph dynamics and high-/low-energy phases also show why emergent space alone does not meet the author's stronger aim.

No established complete solution to the author's question was identified in these sources. This is a targeted follow-up, not an exhaustive literature review or an originality claim.
