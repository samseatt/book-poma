## Added subject matter and design value

- **Norms:** empirical versus normative expectations, reputation, reciprocal obligations, sanctions and pluralistic ignorance. Enforcement itself raises questions of voice and authority.
- **Scale:** Dunbar’s recurring layers and disputed extrapolation; institutions and stories alongside personal relationships. Complex contagion explains why carrying information across a bridge is different from securing risky cooperation.
- **Collective efficacy:** trust plus the willingness to intervene for a common good, distinguished from simple density or friendliness. The Chicago result remains an adjusted association.
- **Structure:** housing, access, work, resources and repeated institutional decisions can reproduce separation. Moving to Opportunity supplies evidence that childhood exposure to place matters. Chetty and colleagues’ 2022 distinction between exposure and friendship formation makes the campus/locals divide more precise.
- **Mediated society:** ranking alters encounters and visible behavior; experimental feed changes do not license every claim about polarization. A human–bot coordination experiment shows a concrete network intervention, and a 2025 LLM naming-game study demonstrates conventions and collective bias under specified conditions. These prepare Volume III without borrowing its whole design program or replacing Interlude 5’s technical work.
- **Visual teaching:** one original grayscale figure contains three parallel network diagrams: bonds, bridges and authority. Same people, different edges. It has a caption and alt text; it is neither a measured LA map nor a proxy for Φ.

The additions are meant to make the stories explanatory rather than merely illustrative. A reader should leave able to distinguish social closeness, collective capacity, authority and the resources needed to turn an encounter into an opportunity. This is an editorial assessment of reader value, not empirical reader testing.

## Repairs and limits

- Durkheim’s interdependence no longer requires universal belief in fair exchange. Marx’s structural conflict no longer ends simply because people refuse. Weber’s legal legitimacy is distinguished from actual impartiality. Bourdieu’s material account is not replaced by ease or manners alone.
- Initiation: the Aronson–Mills experiment measured liking after embarrassment, not durable solidarity; a later fraternity study complicates the hazing rationale. Turner’s communitas is named without claiming every transitional bond is uniquely strong. Flack’s experiment is bounded to one captive pigtailed-macaque troop. The borrowed house is a comparison, not a scientific control group.
- Sosis’s citation now points to the 2003 study with Bressler and records the religious/secular distinction and observational limit. The wording inside held Alice still needs the proposed local repair.
- UCLA/USC remains the author-attended Rose Bowl game, 38–37 on 21 November 1992. An explicit flash-forward prevents readers placing it before the April unrest. The inaccurate ninety-thousand attendance is replaced with “a stadium full”; the overtime claim is restricted to the relevant division's regular season.
- Harlins: March killing, October conviction, November sentencing. Rodney King trial: one deadlocked count is acknowledged. Commencement: 8 May, nine days after the verdict, hence “a little over a week later.”
- Casualty and property totals are stated broadly because sources count differently. “Buildings damaged” is not silently converted into “buildings burned.” Korean American self-defense and failed protection remain without reducing the unrest to two uniformly opposed populations.
- The closing sentence now describes different parts of a city caught in the same catastrophe, not a city demonstrably acquiring a single will. The stronger related Alice claims remain explicitly pending; the note qualifications are not a substitute for that later prose revision.
