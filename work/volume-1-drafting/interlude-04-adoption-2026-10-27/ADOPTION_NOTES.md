## Reader value and allocation

The reader should now be able to trace source code into running state, explain why individually correct actions can fail when combined, distinguish protection from progress, and understand the conventional software inside a training or inference service. The personal cases motivate those mechanisms rather than merely supplying moral lessons about elegance or flexibility.

Interlude 11's incoming draft was read in full to allocate prerequisites. It retains intelligence, learning architectures, transformers, reasoning, alignment and the larger agent synthesis. Interlude 4 supplies the execution machinery on which those discussions depend; Interlude 5 retains the fuller network/distributed-system treatment. The expansion is deliberate and authorized: it supplies the previously missing domain knowledge, not a general length target for future units. The final print-page balance remains a later editorial/publishing check. This reader-value judgment is editorial, not the result of reader testing.
