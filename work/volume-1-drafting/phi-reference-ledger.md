# Φ reference ledger

Source pass completed 4 October 2026 for the two **separate review drafts**. The [chapter](phi-chapter.review.md) and [interlude](phi-interlude.review.md) each contain self-contained native Markdown notes. [phi-sources.bib](phi-sources.bib) supplies 31 stable bibliography records for deliberate later adoption; [phi-source-catalog.json](phi-source-catalog.json) preserves note-to-source mappings, exact checked locators and verification limits.

This is passage-level verification of the drafted claims, not a claim to have reread every cited work in full. Notes cover significant historical, philosophical, scientific and technical material across both complete drafts, including retained material. Ordinary illustrations, the author's argument and explicit speculative extensions are distinguished from sourced findings. A citation to a theory identifies its support and scope; it does not turn a contested interpretation into consensus.

All online sources were accessed on **2026-10-04**. DOI identifiers, where available, are retained in the bibliography and catalog. Full source PDFs are not added to the repository. Reader notes remain usable before the shared Quarto bibliography is populated. No unresolved citation keys are installed in manuscript prose.

## Adoption and remaining editorial work

These sources accompany the review drafts; they have not been merged into `quarto/references.bib`. On adoption, preserve note IDs and substantive qualifications, merge the bibliography deliberately, then use citation keys inside the notes once the publisher has verified the rendering. Do not silently replace a consulted translation with another edition whose wording differs.

The author still owns the truth of the childhood recollections. Any new personal detail added in review must preserve that boundary. Review of the unchanged Prologue/Open, the rest of Volume I, and later technical expansions remains outside this source pass. In particular, the existing Open's unqualified unprovability wording should receive a small scoped repair when the pair is adopted. No additional scene, textbook priority claim, or scientific solution is inferred from these notes.
