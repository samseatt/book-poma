17 November 2026. Authorized after the read-only scene/fit review; Chapter 7 and Interlude 7 supply the working examples. All edits are C (literary additions) explicitly approved by the author. No original paragraph, signature term, heading, joke, scientific claim, annotation or ending was replaced or removed. No new scientific assertion is certified by the literary references.

## Scope and proportion

Eleven short insertions across five chapters and six interludes. Chapter 1 already has the rabbit/watch descent and stays byte-identical. Stronger connections develop the biology/process, psychological evidence, representation, civics and durable-obligations questions. The less exact matches remain short jokes or asides. The allusions enter at different positions rather than imposing a new identical opening on every section.

No full retelling of the Mad Tea-Party, Caucus-race or Cheshire Cat: later Fable Volume II drafts already use these extensively. Interlude 4 uses the pin-prick episode, not the famous memory quotation reserved for Interlude 33. The Caterpillar here concerns metamorphosis; the later scale/mushroom use can develop it differently.

### 02-chapter.md

**Before:** no passage at this insertion point. **After (authorized):**

> In Wonderland, Alice asks the Caterpillar whether becoming a chrysalis and then a butterfly will feel a little queer. "Not a bit," he says.[^v1-c2-carroll] The fellow demanding to know who she is has a fairly drastic change of his own coming. I want to follow what keeps unfolding through those changes.

**Function:** Continuity of a living process through changing form; entry into the existing ripple argument.

**Primary-text check:** Alice asks about chrysalis and butterfly; the Caterpillar answers “Not a bit.” [Source](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rgs/alice-V.html).

### 03-chapter.md

**Before:** no passage at this insertion point. **After (authorized):**

> When Wonderland's cards fly at Alice, she wakes to her sister brushing dead leaves from her face.[^v1-c3-carroll] Carroll leaves the dream and its surroundings touching at that little seam. A leaf doesn't arrive with a queen printed on it. The brain supplies rather a lot.

**Function:** Literary juxtaposition of dream and sensory surroundings, without claiming experimental proof or a causal explanation of the dream.

**Primary-text check:** The attacking pack is followed immediately by waking with her sister brushing fallen leaves from her face. [Source](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rgs/alice-XII.html).

### 04-chapter.md

**Before:** no passage at this insertion point. **After (authorized):**

> The Queen of Hearts wants the sentence before the verdict.[^v1-c4-carroll] A self defending itself can conduct the same proceedings without raising its voice. The more interesting problem is who gets to call a witness.

**Function:** Self-confirming judgment and the later wager about who or what is allowed to correct the self.

**Primary-text check:** The Queen demands sentence before verdict; Alice objects. [Source](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rgs/alice-XII.html).

### 05-chapter.md

**Before:** no passage at this insertion point. **After (authorized):**

> The Hatter's party has a large table, three occupants crowded at one corner, and an announcement that there is no room for Alice.[^v1-c5-carroll] Crowding, apparently, can be a membership policy.

**Function:** Social admission versus physical proximity; reserves the stopped-Time treatment for Chapter 12.

**Primary-text check:** Three occupants at one corner of a large table cry “No room!”; Alice points out the available space. [Source](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rgs/alice-VII.html).

### 06-chapter.md

**Before:** no passage at this insertion point. **After (authorized):**

> The March Hare insists he used the best butter in the Hatter's watch.[^v1-c6-carroll] The watch is two days wrong, but the butter's credentials remain impeccable.

**Function:** A brief joke about assurance checking the wrong property; does not replace the robust-yet-fragile argument.

**Primary-text check:** The Hatter says the watch is two days wrong; the March Hare twice defends the quality of the butter. [Source](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rgs/alice-VII.html).

### 01-interlude.md

**Before:** no passage at this insertion point. **After (authorized):**

> Carroll's bread-and-butterfly has wings of bread and butter, a sugar head, and a dietary requirement for weak tea with cream. If it cannot find that, it dies.[^v1-i1-carroll] A wonderfully short recipe. Keeping it going is another matter.

**Function:** Pattern description versus the conditions of continued existence; a light aside after the existing feedstock qualification.

**Primary-text check:** The Gnat describes bread-and-butter wings, crust body, sugar head, and the diet of weak tea with cream; failure to find food means death. [Source](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Through_the_Looking-Glass,_and_What_Alice_Found_There/Chapter_III).

### 02-interlude.md

**Before:** no passage at this insertion point. **After (authorized):**

> Carroll gives Alice a landscape laid out as a chessboard and offers her a place as a pawn.[^v1-i2-carroll] The Othello learner gets only the moves; the board has to take shape inside. It has found the squares without ever being shown the lawn.

**Function:** World representation learned from moves; the existing next paragraph explicitly keeps subjective experience as the unproved leap.

**Primary-text check:** Alice sees the brooks and hedges forming a chessboard; the Red Queen offers her the role of White Queen’s pawn. [Source](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Through_the_Looking-Glass,_and_What_Alice_Found_There/Chapter_II).

### 03-interlude.md

**Before:** no passage at this insertion point. **After (authorized):**

> The White Knight keeps rain out of his box by carrying it upside down. Alice points out that the open lid lets the contents fall out.[^v1-i3-carroll] I can imagine the specification: waterproof, fully inspectable, sandwiches optional.

**Function:** Light engineering joke about a partial account of success, beside the distinction between inspection and explanation.

**Primary-text check:** The box is carried upside down to exclude rain; its open lid lets clothes and sandwiches escape. The imagined specification is new prose, not Carroll dialogue. [Source](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Through_the_Looking-Glass,_and_What_Alice_Found_There/Chapter_VIII).

### 04-interlude.md

**Before:** no passage at this insertion point. **After (authorized):**

> The White Queen screams before she pricks her finger, then sees no reason to scream once it has happened.[^v1-i4-carroll] An agent can also prepare for something that hasn't happened, but its forecast mustn't quietly become a memory. Otherwise it could finish the apology before doing the damage, and count the matter closed.

**Function:** Anticipation versus history and accountability; leaves the famous backwards-memory quotation for Interlude 33.

**Primary-text check:** The White Queen screams before the pin slips and says afterward that she has already done the screaming. [Source](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Through_the_Looking-Glass,_and_What_Alice_Found_There/Chapter_V).

### 05-interlude.md

**Before:** no passage at this insertion point. **After (authorized):**

> Tweedledum and Tweedledee agree to fight until six and then have dinner.[^v1-i5-carroll] Consensus has done its job; civics has barely begun.

**Function:** Agreement on procedure does not itself settle purpose or civic judgment. Does not claim they actually fight until six.

**Primary-text check:** Tweedledum proposes fighting until six and then dinner; Tweedledee agrees. The crow interrupts before the battle. [Source](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Through_the_Looking-Glass,_and_What_Alice_Found_There/Chapter_IV).

### 06-interlude.md

**Before:** no passage at this insertion point. **After (authorized):**

> Humpty Dumpty makes words mean whatever he chooses, then explains that the question is who gets to be master.[^v1-i6-carroll] A promise that can change its meaning whenever convenient is Humpty Dumpty with an API.

**Function:** The executor must not control the meaning of its own obligations; carries the existing freeze-promises/thaw-procedure wager.

**Primary-text check:** Humpty Dumpty asserts his authority to assign meanings and asks which is to be master. [Source](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Through_the_Looking-Glass,_and_What_Alice_Found_There/Chapter_VI).
