## New optional Fokker connection — source found, not drafted into the book

While checking the café story, a much more personal book-wide thread appeared in Dijkstra's own interview. On p. 42 he recalls installing the FERTA computer for Fokker at Schiphol, calculating wing resonance frequencies, and later assuring an apologetic host after a flight in a Fairchild F27 that he felt safe because he had done those calculations himself. This could connect the Prologue's Fokker, physics, chips and network algorithms unusually well. It does **not** establish that he calculated the particular aircraft Sam flew in, nor a causal connection to Sam's experience.

Source: Phil Frana, [“An Interview with Edsger W. Dijkstra”](https://doi.org/10.1145/1787234.1787249), *CACM* 53(8) (2010): 41–47, especially p. 42; recorded August 2001. [Readable article scan](https://www.eng.auburn.edu/~vagrawal/COURSE/READING/ARCH/Interview_Dijkstra.pdf). No quotation or mini-biography has been added to the interlude: this is an author choice, not a reason to resume journalist-style history-of-science narration.
