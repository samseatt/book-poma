## Boundaries and follow-up

- All reference uses have definitions; no new source is cited in a figure caption. The caption reference/backlink failure found in earlier passes is avoided.
- Imported source-note/AI-draft comments are preserved for provenance. The new adjacent audit comment supersedes their pending-from-memory status for this adoption.
- Links sometimes restrict automated access (notably some author/publisher pages). Where direct open failed but indexed primary passages were available, the verified passage was used with the audit limited accordingly. Books used only for course context are metadata checks, not falsely claimed full-text reviews.
- No clinical, legal or public-policy prescription is inferred from the agent-civics analogy. Its empirical and normative questions remain open in the speculation ledger.
