## What remains open

Economic curvature and time-inflation are authorial research offers, not established metrics or claimed priority discoveries. A useful test would compare economies or networks with similar resources and inequality but different ownership, dependency, portability and exit arrangements. Does the proposed measure explain attainable options beyond existing market-power, network and credit models? Can richer accounting improve coordination without imposing one global value function or concentrating control of the records? These obligations are recorded in speculation-ledger entry S028.

The first Alice's best insight survives in the persistence of claims after their originating circumstances disappear. The wider forgetting-curve prescription is not imported. The separate first attempt remains untouched in the incoming tree.

The two supplied figures are included locally so this reading copy resolves its illustrations. **Only the Gordon payment wording was changed in a drawing.** The equal-rules simulation and the rest of the chapter's older sources still require their normal full-merge audit; this focused revision does not certify them.
