## Outstanding evidence

Russell's stopped-clock passage requires a primary edition check; the 1948 bibliographic lead is plausible, but a search result is not a checked passage. Frege's original 1902 correspondence/1903 appendix and Hilbert's 1930 radio-address text/exact meeting chronology remain open. The substantive review flags these explicitly. No new source has been fabricated to close a placeholder.

The primary sources above establish the technical constructions and documented episodes. They do not establish the author's prephysical possibility conjecture, a universal law of emergence, a theorem about substrate unknowability, or a causal mechanism merely because its prediction succeeds.
