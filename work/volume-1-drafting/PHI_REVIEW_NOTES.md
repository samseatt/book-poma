## What changed

The chapter continues directly from the Prologue's basket. It develops experience, memory, language, knowledge and the difference between certainty and warrant, then makes logic useful before arriving at the foundations crisis and Gödel. Its compass also needs a bearing: what would count as genuine advance, and who chooses that end? Repeated introductions of the arrow, basket, castle and monster have been consolidated. Their strongest functions remain available to the later book rather than being repeatedly explained at the entrance.

The interlude keeps the actual childhood questions and turns them into a progression through representation, formal structure, proof, computability, measurement, scientific inquiry, uncertainty and implemented logic. New worked examples give the ideas something to test: absent versus empty data, a valid inference and its invalid converse, systematic measurement error, and a detector's base rate. Learning's cooperation with formal proof replaces the inherited categorical separation. Detailed ML architecture is left for later assessment, principally against Interlude 11; the original material remains in the untouched canonical source.

The chapter owns the drama and philosophical consequence of Gödel; the interlude owns the mechanism and conditions. Knowledge, logic and models appear in both where they perform different work. The brief quantum connection remains an openly philosophical line of inquiry, grounded in physical registration and distinguished from subjective experience. The text neither makes consciousness create matter nor claims to solve the measurement problem.

The book's orientation is expressed as **Φ**; the empty-set symbol **∅** is reserved for actual mathematics. Chapter's tertiary label is **ORIENTATION**. The interlude keeps **The Edges of Reason**, with the revised subtitle **From Symbols to the Silicon Mirror** and domain label **INQUIRY** to accommodate both formal and empirical work. These title choices belong to this review draft and have not been installed in the index or publisher configuration.
