## Reader value

Afterward an educated reader should be able to explain how a partial cue recruits a recollection without certifying its source; distinguish felt agency from control and free will; describe how appraisal changes a response; and separate understanding another person from sharing their feeling or helping them. The cases also teach why experimental findings, clinical categories, legal responsibility and philosophical positions answer different questions.

This is a useful conceptual introduction to psychological personhood, with mechanisms beyond slogans. It does not comprehensively teach development, psychometrics or clinical treatment. The judgment is editorial, not reader testing. Alice remains a research invitation, and the author's final literary pass remains welcome.
