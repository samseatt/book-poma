## Distinctions carried into the research note

- The opening first-person passage is a proposed articulation of Sam’s conjecture. It is not claimed as the conclusion of the cited papers.
- The selected lines of research share motivations and occasional mathematics; their ontologies, probability interpretations and retained structures differ. No unified derivation has been established.
- The formulas for relative Boltzmann weights and for the logarithm of a full-rank Gibbs density operator are elementary consequences of their stated equilibrium premises. Their interpretation as an origin mechanism remains open.
- The coin-flip and two-state quantum examples are explanatory calculations. They are not empirical measurements or simulations.
- Repeated state-space vocabulary does not establish identity among classical alternatives, quantum amplitudes and prephysical possibilities.
- The physics corrections distinguish mathematical consistency, experimental evidence, model-dependent results and philosophical interpretation. No claim is made that all foundational questions can be answered within today’s formalism.

## Leads deliberately not promoted into evidence

Wheeler’s participatory or information-first ideas, Bohm’s implicate order and quantum potential, Wigner’s reflections on mathematics, and broader modal realism may be useful in a later history-of-ideas passage. They should receive their own primary-text comparison before being presented as predecessors of this precise conjecture. No priority claim is made for the author’s synthesis.

The exact Einstein “same thinking” quotation remains unverified and is not used as an attributed quotation. “RQD” in the author’s prompt was not assigned a specific expansion. “Planck” and “Calabi–Yau” are used in the research prose; no author manuscript spelling was changed.

Some web searches returned unrelated material; these results were excluded. One mistakenly targeted quantum-optics paper, arXiv:quant-ph/0607023, was identified as irrelevant and is not used. Failed source fetches were not treated as proof of content. The bibliography is selective, not an exhaustive review of foundations or quantum gravity.
