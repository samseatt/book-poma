## Bob’s philosophical direction

The author explicitly authorized a substantial revision of Bob. Its stronger question is the gap between operational inspection and explanation: knowing the steps does not necessarily tell us what useful organization learning has produced. This retains the knobs payoff, layered grids, the 1985 FPGA connection and the possibility of livewired hardware.

The new physical foothold is conductance encoding and current summation in analog in-memory computation. The larger question is whether learning could revise where the architecture places its abstractions, including some of its physical organization. Present research supports limited physical computation and training, not continuously growing general intelligence. Noise, conversion, stability and total-system costs keep the challenge concrete. The speculative link to confounder holes remains an invitation to measurement, not a new discovery attributed to existing research.

No major literary idea or coincidence was cut. The discarded assertions were the previously identified factual overclaims: perfect transparency, computing in silicon from its beginning, software riding above hardware as a new invention, all neural layers being matrix products, GPUs as extreme RISC, universal memory starvation and brain efficiency explained merely by obeying nature. These were replaced by better arguments. Repeated versions of the memory commute and the transparent-mind slogan were consolidated. The closing cortex/motherboard sentence and Alex’s fixed-wiring claim received local repairs; their narrative functions remain.

## Reader value

An educated reader should now be able to follow an instruction into a physical state change, explain why several memory tiers exist, and reason about why a neural workload benefits from a GPU—or fails to achieve its advertised throughput. She should distinguish biological timing from conventional clocked sequencing without treating the distinction as absolute. Bob then asks what those mechanisms leave unexplained.

The main text grows from about 5,000 incoming words to about 5,800, chiefly for causal explanation and captions. There is no whole-unit polish pass over the author's voice. This is an editorial assessment, not evidence from reader testing. The author remains free to extend or replace Bob’s conjecture.
