## What changed substantively

The “Silicon Tonnage Tax” remains a useful provocative name, but the middle no longer treats compute, freight, resource burden, job displacement and captured value as interchangeable measures. It separates infrastructure charges, environmental limits/charges, income/profit/rent/wealth taxation, and returns from shared ownership. Incidence, authority, data burden and avoidance must be examined. The original opening and closing still contain the older combined rhetoric; they are explicitly reserved and must be reconciled before final drafting/publication. Tax design should support beneficial automation rather than preserve drudgery to protect payroll receipts.

The builder material adds a functioning path, not only a list of vetoes. Valid decisions execute. Legitimate amendments take effect. Essential services have previously authorized continuity. Unresolved authority returns to the competent body instead of silently becoming approval or a final denial of rights. A technical quorum is not a political majority, an authentic credential is not legal authority, and a rule translation is not a transfer of jurisdiction.

Private-agent permission belongs primarily to pair 24; commons resource management to 27; production qualification to 28; remedy to 30; economic claims to 25. Pair 29 draws on them to address public and collective authority, without absorbing their chapters.

## Intellectual thread and experimental work

Primary opportunity signals remain **S18 and S20**; supporting **S13, S16, S19, S21 and S22**, per the existing design map. The pair turns those concerns into institutional placement, disciplined coordination, correction and continuity. No incoming Volume II draft is adopted.

**Alice — Can Intelligence Distribute Power?** asks whether stronger machine-assisted coordination can enlarge the feasible scale of polycentric agency without concentrating unaccountable control in the intermediary. Existing polycentric governance is prior art. The experiment measures useful service, effective power, participation burden, dissent and replaceability, including withdrawal of founder or provider. Neither originality nor success is asserted.

**Bob — Can a Rule Survive Its Translation?** proposes a translation dossier exposing preserved obligations, assumptions, counterexamples and undecidable questions. Its experimental target is semantic interoperability across differently governed institutions without universal-model domination. Formal methods and testing are established foundations; political agreement cannot be proved into existence by a compiler.

**The Next Move — Put One Rule Within Reach** asks the reader to help an affected person bring an evidence-based amendment into an actual decision process with implementation resources and an accountable response. The measure is usable co-authorship, not automatic victory for the reader's proposal.
