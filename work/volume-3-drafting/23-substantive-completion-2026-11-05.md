## Alice and Bob — working wagers for Sam

These are new, explicitly exploratory drafts, not claims of priority or empirical discovery. Their titles are provisional; final volume-specific Alice/Bob motifs remain open.

**Alice: The Knot That Can Loosen.** Can cooperation strengthen while dependence on its original coordinating centre decreases, without transferring hidden work onto vulnerable people or excluding newcomers? The established ideas of distributed cooperation and accountable institutions are footing, not the proposed novelty. The interesting test concerns a development trajectory in which participant capability and replaceability grow together.

**Bob: Can the Coordinator Learn to Step Aside?** Can learning tools propose and help test changes to the distribution of coordination itself, under participant authority, instead of merely optimizing tasks inside the existing arrangement? Ordinary automation is insufficient evidence. Tests must include burden, access, recourse and indispensable privileges/knowledge, not just removal of a software component.

These wagers connect to Sam's situated-agency philosophy and remain available for his stronger original twists. They neither claim a view outside the system nor attribute his extension to Nagel. They do not require every useful institution to disappear. Pair 33 still owns full succession/release teaching; here the theme is introduced through the smallest working dependency.
