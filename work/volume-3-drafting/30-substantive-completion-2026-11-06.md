# Pair 30 — Justice as a civilization's capacity to improve and repair itself

6 November 2026 · Substantive source completed for author review · Literary projections and final cadences reserved

## The governing change

Sam's instruction is an inversion, not a request to modernize a courthouse. Begin with flourishing, usable freedom, trustworthy relationships and ecological fit; design conditions that prevent avoidable harm and support repair. Jurisprudence, accountability, proportionate protection and sanctions occupy a necessary but smaller part of this scope. The new source must not be compressed back into a list of prohibitions on AI or an appeals-interface chapter.

Quality assurance is a useful engineering ally. Justice also questions the purpose and specification being assured. The target of improvement is the arrangement and its consequences, not conformity of people to an optimizer's preferred population. This distinction keeps the ambitious civilizational design from becoming either conventional legal reform or therapeutic authoritarianism.

**Chapter 30 — Justice Before Judgment: Living Inside Fair Systems (JUSTICE)** now develops capabilities and provision, upstream alternatives to forced choices, C8 and fair crossing among plural orders, requirements traceability, decision tables, statistical fairness tradeoffs, causal analysis with accountability, one route through repair, class-wide correction, proportionate prevention and a research wager about revising the system's own questions.

The tertiary title changes from **JURISPRUDENCE** to **INTEGRITY**; the two literary titles and filenames stay fixed. The temporary-address example supplies a concrete thread without claiming an actual agency uses these rules. It demonstrates prevention, legitimate continuity, correction and changed conditions. Broader examples include pharmacy, journalism, provisioning, transactions and ecology; the point is not to annex those domains to a new supreme justice office. Each retains expertise and authority while owing something across the whole chain.
