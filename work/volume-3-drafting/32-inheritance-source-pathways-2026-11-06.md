# Pair 32 — source pathways for living inheritance

6 November 2026. Working research map for Sam/Claude; not a comparative ranking of religions, a complete account of any tradition, or a finalized plan for the eleven Opens. Source texts are paraphrased in the chapter. Applications to future systems are our proposed interpretations, not meanings retrospectively asserted as the texts' original technical intent.

## Religious and philosophical starting points

| Tradition / source | What the source supports | Productive direction and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Jewish: *Leviticus* 19:18, 33–34 ([Sefaria](https://www.sefaria.org/Leviticus.19.34)) | Neighbor-love and a specific duty toward the resident stranger, with remembered strangerhood | Extend belonging and care across convenient boundaries. Do not pretend the scope of these duties has had one uncontested interpretation. |
| Christian: *Luke* 10:25–37 ([KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+10%3A25-37&version=KJV)) | Samaritan intervention across an inherited division; neighborliness realized in conduct | An agent's practical help to an outsider is a stronger test than its religious vocabulary. The service architecture is our application. |
| Muslim: *Qur'an* [49:13](https://quran.com/49/13), [55:7–9](https://legacy.quran.com/55/7-9) | Mutual knowing among peoples; balance and just measure | Plural integration and restraint. Work with Muslim interpreters; neither a network specification nor a proof that scripture contains climate science. |
| Hindu: *Bhagavad Gita* 3:20–25 ([IIT Kanpur 3.20](https://heritage.iitk.ac.in/SBG/3.20); [Prabhupāda's distinct reading](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/3/20/)) | Action in relation to *loka-saṅgraha*, variously rendered through maintaining/welfare/guidance of the world | Competence exercised for more than private gain. Retain the text's wider context and different interpretive schools. Do not generalize one verse into all Hindu thought. |
| Buddhist: *Sutta Nipāta* 1.8 ([Ṭhānissaro translation](https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/KN/StNp/StNp1_8.html)) | Cultivation of goodwill toward beings beyond one's favorites | Attention and conduct beyond in-group reward. The translator explicitly warns against misreading the mother/child comparison; no need to reproduce that image here. |
| Jain: *Tattvārtha Sūtra* 5.21 ([Vijay K. Jain translation/commentary](https://www.wisdomlib.org/jainism/book/tattvartha-sutra-with-commentary/d/doc1084775.html)) | Mutual support of *jīvas* within Jain metaphysics | Interdependence and responsibility as resources for design, without relabelling the text as modern ecological science. |
| Sikh: closing *Japji*, *Guru Granth Sahib*, Ang 8 ([text reproduced by Yale](https://fore.yale.edu/World-Religions/Sikhism/Sacred-Texts)) | Sacred relations of air, water and earth | Planetary obligation from a living religious source. Paraphrase rather than silently reuse a modern copyrighted translation. |
| Daoist: *Daodejing* 67 ([Legge 1891](https://sacred-texts.com/book/the-texts-of-taoism-part-i-of-ii/chapter-67)) | Gentleness, economy and reluctance to put oneself first in this translation | Restraint, capable non-domination and questioning extraction. Other translations choose different words; select and identify the edition if later quoting. |
| Confucian: *Analects* 13.23 ([Chinese and Legge](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/4094/4094-h/4094-h.htm)) | *Hé ér bù tóng*, often rendered as harmony without sameness; Legge uses affability versus adulation | A fruitful plural-integration theme, with translation differences disclosed. Not a claim that historical Confucian institutions were uniformly pluralistic. |
| Zoroastrian: *Yasna* 30.2 ([Avesta archive](https://www.avesta.org/yasna/yasna.htm)) | Listening and considered choice within the Gatha's religious cosmology | A Persian-thread return to deliberation. Avoid turning it into a modern scientific-method quotation or assigning a speculative founding date. |
| Secular / atheist participation | Sam's explicit stance and proposed cooperative work | Shared action does not require shared metaphysics. The atheist olive branch is not a hidden conversion program in either direction. |

These are starting points, not coverage claims for every religion or every branch. Future additions can include other traditions where they contribute a substantive connection, rather than satisfy a headcount.

## Technical and design grounding

- **Value sensitive design:** [the originating research lab's method](https://vsdesign.org/vsd/). Conceptual, empirical and technical investigations; direct/indirect stakeholders; multi-lifespan thinking. The civilizational/religious applications are proposed.
- **AI alignment:** [Gabriel 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.09768) distinguishes targets of alignment and the problem of fair legitimacy. [Bai et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073) demonstrates a principle-guided training approach. Neither establishes machine conscience or the efficacy of the pair's reciprocal cultural-learning wager.
- **Semantic continuity:** [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/), [SKOS](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/) §10, [ODRL](https://www.w3.org/TR/odrl-model/), [OAIS 2024](https://ccsds.org/Pubs/650x0m3.pdf) §4.3.2.3, [PREMIS3](https://www.loc.gov/standards/premis/v3/premis-3-0-final.pdf). These describe representations and preservation machinery, not truth or moral correctness.
- **Technical date anchors:** [Grolier/Princeton's Aldus Virgil, April 1501](https://grolierclub.omeka.net/items/show/186); [DOE's Human Genome Project chronology](https://doe-humangenomeproject.ornl.gov/human-genome-project-timeline/), specifying the 15/16 February 2001 issue dates. The former is not the invention of small books; the latter is not a complete explanation of life.

## Opens and quotation work still to do

The author intends sayings from varied traditions across the eleven V3 Opens, including Mao. This pass does not select or insert those quotations. Mao should be identified as a secular political thinker/leader, not a religion, and not a proxy for all Chinese people. Confucian, Daoist, Buddhist and other Chinese traditions are distinct. A selected saying can be valuable without endorsing its speaker's whole political record.

When selecting final wording, verify the passage, edition, translator, attribution, context and usable quotation length/rights. Do not pass a contemporary paraphrase off as ancient verbatim speech. The source pathways above support the current prose; they do not grant blanket permission to reproduce modern translations.
