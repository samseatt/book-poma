## Chapter 24 — Recovered time, already spoken for

### The proposed story

Sam thinks toward September 2031 and the kind of ordinary freedom he would like his work to have helped create. By this point, he expects capable agents to take whole pieces of coordination off people's shoulders. His own helper has made an attractive arrangement possible: a modest continuing commitment to mentor people finding new ways to contribute as their professions change. It has understood the proposed work, examined the relevant material, matched actual needs to his experience, prepared useful alternatives and negotiated a manageable commitment with the other participants' representatives. Sam has chosen the commitment and its limits. He can now show up and be useful without first becoming the unpaid administrator of his usefulness.

That success gives the helper's next suggestion weight. It has found room for more mentoring by recommending less time for his slow, speculative writing. The comparison is intelligent, generous and quite possibly correct on its stated terms. The people who need help are real within the projection; the benefits of another afternoon are easier to establish than the benefits of another unwieldy paragraph about civilization. The comic wound is recognition. Sam could have made the same argument to somebody else. Now a remarkably capable representative is making it to him, with fewer digressions.

He keeps the useful arrangement and declines the expansion. He wants to remain available to people without turning all recovered time into available capacity. His writing need not win a social-return contest to remain his choice. Nor must the machine pretend his choice has no cost: fewer mentoring places may genuinely remain. The hoped-for accomplishment is a form of belonging in which a person can accept that consequence, keep his commitment, retain his standing and continue receiving useful assistance. He helped build something that can take his contribution without taking over its author.

The ending can be small: an afternoon remains his, and the existing commitment still works. No explanatory victory speech is needed. The narrator who wants civilization to become more capable has finally encountered some spare capacity and, disgracefully, intends to keep a little.

### How to unfold it

1. **Enter through expectation.** Something like “When I think toward that September, I hope to have become easier to help” gives Sam a thought to pursue, rather than requiring a room, weather, a screen animation and a cast. His prospective report can then acquire present-tense immediacy. Use the Gate date once where it earns its ceremonial place.
2. **Let the help earn trust first.** Show one accomplished chain, not a catalogue of services. The mentoring arrangement is the proposed chain: reading and evaluating the work, reconciling participants' needs, negotiating the bounded role, and leaving Sam an intelligible commitment. Routine reminders alone would make this an unduly small picture of 2031 capability.
3. **Deliver the almost-right recommendation.** Retain the old opening's recognizable pressure on speculative writing. The helper may offer a serious argument, with uncertainties, rather than an absurd confidence score for the worth of a life. Its challenge can be uncomfortable without making it malicious or foolish.
4. **Make the contribution visible through the response.** The Neuverse-side milestone is that this small participating network has learned to accommodate bounded contribution. A refusal does not automatically become a character defect shared with other services. Sam is one participant using an arrangement he has helped develop, not the sovereign who grants himself an exemption.
5. **Land on the time still available.** The broader question grows from that modest outcome: how can help enlarge a person's feasible life without quietly selecting its destination?

### Why this belongs here

**“Agency, Trust, and Life Inside the New Mesh”** becomes a lived bargain. Sam trusts the helper because it delivers; he belongs because he can contribute; he retains agency because the arrangement can accept a boundary. The tension concerns the purpose of help, beyond whether the recommendation is accurate. This is the human reason for the chapter's distinctions between preference, permission, institutional rule and an inferred model of a person.

The title *The Citizen's Dilemma* gains an extra sting: the demand comes partly from Sam's own desire to be a good citizen. No external villain is required. Collective needs can be compelling without conferring a claim on every remaining hour. This keeps the argument relevant to a future Neusphere without declaring that a citywide Neusphere has already emerged.

The historical braid then widens the problem: Darius's administrative capacity around **500 BCE** made people more legible and provision more effective, while the **499 BCE** revolt exposes the limits of belonging on imperial terms. The **1801** institutional transfer introduces the possibility of changing who exercises authority without destroying the arrangement. These are distinct historical lessons; neither needs to be made an exact analogue of personal software.

### Preserve and reconnect

- Preserve the old opening's **almost-right helper**, its awkwardly persuasive argument, and the narrator's implication in the values he questions. “Low immediate social return” remains a useful phrase precisely because “immediate” carries an unnoticed decision.
- Keep available the existing closing instruction: **“This work is slow because it is trying to change what future systems count. Do not mistake latency for low return.”** Its force is the author's declaration of purpose, not evidence that his work must eventually succeed. “But ask again in a year” can retain revisability without making him reapply annually for permission to write.
- Rework the closing scene alongside the opening. The chapter should return to the bounded commitment and recovered time, rather than to three role cards that no longer appeared. **Keep a Key** can then be concrete: Sam has used help, revised its remit and kept a choice alive.
- Remove obsolete invented continuity when the new opening is installed: the kettle's alleged 2029 incident, the citywide regional mesh's unestablished reach, and any implication that this is a completed autobiographical event. No new flashback is necessary. Sam may supply one genuine memory of being over-useful or having his priorities chosen for him.

The earlier music-session idea remains an available variation. If Sam has a real curiosity he wants to carry forward, an unproductive afternoon learning it may be warmer than another writing deadline. It should replace the closing use of time, not add a second subplot. No musical interest or remembered encounter is assumed here.

## Interlude 24 — A successor without a succession crisis

### The proposed story

Sam projects a quieter builder milestone: he can change the personal delegate working through Tiers while useful work is already under way. The current one is competent and has done nothing scandalous. A different helper suits the next phase of his work, and he wants to change. By 2031, repeated improvements in models, tools and providers make this an ordinary enough wish. What would make it progress for his own project is that an ordinary participant can do it without losing the arrangements built around the old helper—or summoning the founder to repair them privately.

Use the chapter's modest mentoring commitment as one thread through the handover. A session is already agreed; a second request is still being discussed; the boundary around his writing time remains in force. The new helper receives the authorized task history and commitments. Other participants can recognize that Sam is still the person making the promise, although different software now represents him. The old helper's power to make further commitments ends at the agreed handover point. Private exploratory notes do not become part of a compulsory personality export.

The agents can do the demanding migration work: reconcile records, explain mismatches, propose mappings between the services and prepare the new arrangement. One consequential distinction needs Sam's judgment. A note such as “prefer fewer interruptions” cannot silently replace the narrower instruction that protects a particular stretch of writing time; nor can a broad “help me contribute” reopen every declined invitation. He confirms the intended boundary in ordinary language. The technical machinery must then carry the resulting limit to the places where actions can actually be taken. Some participating services may require a fresh grant or explicit acceptance of the handover; this is handled as part of the transition, not concealed by an export button.

The result worth reporting is continuity with a different representative. The appointment survives, the pending request is honestly accounted for, and neither helper has acquired the right to promise the afternoon away. Sam can be pleased by a system that has become less dependent on the system he previously chose. There is room for self-mockery in a builder discovering that a respectable measure of his infrastructure is how easily someone can stop using one of its favoured components.

### How to unfold it

1. **Begin with the ordinary wish to change.** No contrived emergency, corrupt provider or combative review meeting is needed. The old helper's competence makes replaceability more interesting: dependence can accumulate through excellent service.
2. **Give the change something to carry.** The agreed mentoring session and unresolved invitation make continuity tangible. Moving a blank account proves little. The stakes are promises made to other people, useful context and limits that still have to mean the same thing.
3. **Choose one revealing mismatch.** Use the writing-time boundary rather than a procession of technical faults. The new helper's interpretation may be reasonable yet insufficient. The point is to settle the person's intended bargain and enforce the settled portion, not to claim software can prove perfect equivalence between every human meaning.
4. **Show the accomplishment through ordinary continuity.** The next session remains arranged, Sam can see who is now authorized, and the former helper cannot make a new commitment. Put the machinery's explanation in the interlude body. The opening needs only enough of it to make the success intelligible.
5. **Let the builder encounter his own principle.** If a participant must appeal to Sam's personal goodwill to leave or change representation, he has preserved his own benevolent authority. The projected advance is a supported handover that works without that favour.

### Why this belongs here

**“Building Without Becoming the New Tyrant”** concerns the builder's success as much as his mistakes. Good software can become difficult to leave because relationships, history and authority have accumulated around it. The milestone is a capability that remains usable while its implementation changes. This supplies a concrete entrance to grants, enforcement, delegation, revocation, portability and the Strategy pattern; it also leads naturally to Bob's question of whether a refusal survives translation.

Keep the **1801 Jacquard** paragraph, including the same-year Jefferson connection, when rebuilding the bridge. The old room-bound sentences about an antique loom hiding behind a design meeting will no longer fit. A more useful connection is that executable instructions carry choices into repeated actions. Changing the intelligence that interprets them should not quietly change who gets to make those choices. An adaptive agent can revise plans; a punched-card mechanism did not possess that discretion. The distinction makes the comparison sharper.

The developer oath can remain, with its emotional return redirected toward the handover. Noēsis might dwell on the relief of being supported without becoming irremovable from the supporting machinery. Leave the final meditation and Open to Claude/Sam. Do not duplicate the chapter's long debate about suppressing creative work in a second fictional meeting.

## Why September 2031 is a credible home

The date marks an expected state of use and a proposed project accomplishment. It does not announce the invention of agents or authorization. By this checkpoint, it is reasonable for the book to project much stronger sustained assistance: expert synthesis, substantial production, negotiation within assigned terms, and coordination across participating services. The openings should allow that competence to be impressive. Their central problems persist even if the agents are more intelligent than these examples require.

The harder project assumption is that enough independently responsible participants and services can recognize the arrangements, keep commitments and accept changes of representative. Tiers' proposed achievement is a bounded, usable implementation among connected participants. Universal institutional acceptance, all of Calgary participating, perfect transfer of a person's inner life and an economically self-sustaining Neuconomy are unnecessary to either story. A capable agent cannot create another organization's agreement merely by understanding what should happen.

The five years since the September 2026 launch allow these to be ambitious progress checkpoints. The sequence is intelligible without making a precise delivery forecast:

| Gate | Distinct milestone story |
| --- | --- |
| **March 2029 / I23** | The builder deliberately disturbs an integrated arrangement and learns whether it can recover or honestly expose a limit. |
| **September 2031 / I24** | A participant can change representation during useful ongoing work while commitments and the agreed limits of authority remain meaningful. |
| **March 2034 / I25** | Sufficiently broad participation and internal value creation support the deliberately modest commercial-pizza outcome. The payment mechanism remains Sam's open design question. |

This framing also remains usable under faster technological progress. More powerful models can make the delegation more ambitious; they do not eliminate questions about who may promise what on whose behalf. Under slower integration, the stories can concern a smaller functioning network. Neither adjustment requires making the intelligence artificially inept.

### Evidence behind the projection

- [METR's task-completion time horizons](https://metr.org/time-horizons/) document advancing performance on demanding, primarily software, machine-learning and cybersecurity tasks. The measure uses human task duration; it is not the time an agent works uninterrupted, and the tasks are cleaner than much real-world work. This supports taking substantial agent capability seriously. It does not establish the proposed 2031 social adoption or project outcomes.
- [NIST's February 2026 identity and authority concept paper announcement](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/02/new-concept-paper-identity-and-authority-software-agents) identifies agent identification, authorization, auditing and related controls as an active practical problem. The proposed interlude develops that already-existing problem into a later lived capability. The announcement is not a certification or evidence that semantic handover is solved.
- The pair's canonical source notes cover the existing authorization, delegation and revocation mechanisms. Those mechanisms support the builder explanation; the preservation of a person's intended meaning across changing helpers remains a design and research burden.

Both external pages checked on 5 November 2026. The future constructions are editorial extrapolations, not findings attributed to those sources.
