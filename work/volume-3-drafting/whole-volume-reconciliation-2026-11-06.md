The older opportunity-signal/Next Move maps remain useful inputs, but the new inventory describes the current manuscript substance.

The review compared the existing substantive sections and worked examples across all 24 units, consulted the specified Neuverse and Tiers sources, checked historical/Gate hinges and cross-pair repetitions, and revisited the earlier creative-frontier agenda. This is a substantive editorial reconciliation, not a new sentence-by-sentence scientific certification of the entire volume, a reader study, or evidence that the proposed systems have been built.

The important adjustment is positive as well as protective: greater machine capability should make new arrangements possible. Neusphere is a proposed capacity for connected cognition, not merely another archive or feed; service composition can dissolve unnecessary departmental journeys; joint agency can enlarge what people can do together. The later pairs already contain stronger changes of category—justice as system quality and repair, care as intervention in causal systems, inheritance as living practice and reality as a constraint on even our most virtual constructions. Those are carried backward into the opening discipline without turning every chapter into the same argument.
