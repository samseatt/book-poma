My judgment on the alignment
This is strong enough to use.

The main reason I would not change the chapter order again is that the reordered sequence gives the future “Empire of Speed” a very clean causal escalation:

fracture → agency → economy → truth → substrate → logistics → governance → justice → care → memory → release

That is a powerful future arc.

The Persian timeline still works as a deeper ideological echo:

tribal awakening → imperial order → liberation → syncretic meaning → orthodoxy/enclosure → redistribution/reform → administrative rebirth → ethical kingship/story → catastrophic healing → durable identity → humane civic pause

That is also powerful, even if not every slot is literal.

The two weakest-but-usable matches
Ch 25 with 250 BC Parthian Catalyst This is not “economy” in the narrow sense, but it works if you frame it as value sovereignty after capture: the reclaiming of circulation, identity, and native agency after a foreign imperial overlay.

Ch 30 with 1001 CE Shahnameh At first glance, Shahnameh wants Chapter 32. But it can support Chapter 30 if you frame it as the archive of kingly justice, rightful rule, betrayal, judgment, legitimacy, and the moral education of power. Then Chapter 32 can still take 1501 because Safavid identity formation is a stronger “continuity under durable boundary-making” moment.
