## Prologue and Coda

**Prologue:** The woven toy basket reassures the child during the uncertain flight and family crossing. The remembered aircraft and exact gesture retain their stated uncertainty. Its closing movement makes the basket something lost, remade and eventually offered onward. The Astyages-to-early-Medes rewind can rhyme with the Fokker-to-origins structure without explaining the device to the reader.

**Coda:** The ladder folds back on itself, revealing a missing civic capacity to think together. Castlegar becomes a modest lived glimpse of shared memory, care and practical knowledge. Neusphere names a plural cognitive commons between values and ecological limits; it does not erase cultural mosaics or replace people. This supports the author's present clarification that Neusphere is a civilizational ideal enabled by technology, rather than a technology product. Its own provisional language should survive later tightening.

## The strongest returns to consider first

1. **Renewal:** the flight/rewind structure and the already planted Romulus reference. Do not add every origin myth to the opening.
2. **Agency:** lived exit and exclusion, fallible perception, and the difference between being administered and being able to act.
3. **Economy:** precarious livelihood and the explicit Neuconomy promise. Use the bison counterpoint to expose a different mechanism of provision and coordination.
4. **Minds:** transmitted meaning, Wikipedia and the distance between a person's experience and the public representation of it.
5. **Commons:** the hockey recollection and ecological home. Read Chapter 14's relevant passages before claiming to fulfill its hidden-cultures thread.
6. **Making:** father/engineering/materiality, the constraints under real systems and repair after failure.
7. **Governance:** borders, permission, federation and amendable rules; decentralized architecture alone never establishes human agency.
8. **Justice:** structural harm and being classified by an institution. Shahnameh → geopolitical dispute → individual trial supplies a change of scale, not a proof by analogy.
9. **Care:** Chapter 2's war ending and Chapter 11's grief and healthcare. Distinguish actual historical injury from the prospective danger addressed in 1976.
10. **Continuity:** inherited memory, lost monuments, institutional adaptation after 9/11 and living cultural transmission. Reserve genome material for the interlude if it earns its place.
11. **Release:** the basket offered onward, the machine that repeats inherited incentives, and the Coda's unfinished cognitive commons. Keep the final passenger-to-plane transformation for the Epilogue.

These are available connections, not a callback quota. Often one recognizable detail will carry more than an explanatory paragraph.
